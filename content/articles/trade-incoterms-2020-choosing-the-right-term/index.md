# INCOTERMS 2020 实务：FOB/CIF/DDP 选错要赔钱

你做出口生意多年，可合同上 FOB、CIF、DDP 那行字母，很多时候是照抄客户模板、抄完心里也没底。货在海上被水湿损了，客户回头索赔，你才发现"风险到底谁承担"根本没写清楚；运费暴涨、清关卡壳，买单的还是你。INCOTERMS 2020 不是贸易律师的术语游戏，它直接决定货损风险在哪一刻转移、运费保险归谁、清关责任落在哪头。选错一只术语，很可能就是一笔收不回的货款或一张大额索赔单。搞懂它，比事后补救省太多。

## 第一步：抓住术语的核心：风险、费用、义务三分

每个贸易术语都回答三件事：**风险什么时候转移**、**费用谁承担**、**义务谁履行**。判断一套术语是否适合你，别只看"到哪交货"，要看货损风险点在哪一刻交出去、运费保险费是否会被视为你默示承担。这是选用一切术语的基础。

## 第二步：分清常见术语的差别

- **EXW（工厂交货）**：风险在工厂交货时即转移，买方负责全部运输，卖方责任最小，但对卖方收款与货权约束最弱；\\
- **FOB（船上交货）**：卖方负责装船并承担至装船的费用与风险，运费归买方；\\
- **CIF（成本、保险加运费）**：卖方承担运费并按约投保，风险在装船港装船后转移给买方；\\
- **DDP（完税后交货）**：卖方承担全部运费、保险、清关与进口税费直到目的地交货，责任最重；\\
- 具体到"清关谁做、保单归谁、从哪个港口风险转移"，务必以 INCOTERMS 2020 原文与合同约定为准。

## 第三步：结合追收场景选用术语

- 若你更担心**客户拒付或货权失控**，选用"风险尽早转移、货权尽早交割"的术语，并配套凭证（提单、装箱单、报关单）保存证据；\\
- 若你更担心**货损索赔责任**，明确风险转移时点与保险责任，避免把货损风险默认留给自己；\\
- 大额订单建议同时约定货款结算方式（如信用证、部分预付），避免"货已发出、钱没收齐"的被动局面；\\
- 术语选择不是越"对卖方有利"越好，而要看谁能最有效控制对应环节的风险。

## 时间与费用预期

| 环节 | 影响 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| 术语选择 | 即时决定 | 决定风险、运费、保险、清关责任归属 |
| 合同谈定 | 数天至数周 | 需与术语、付款方式、管辖条款一并敲定 |
| 履约与收付款 | 随交易周期 | 术语影响取证与追收时的责任判断 |

## 风险提示

- 只写"FOB/CIF"却不写明确港口与风险转移点，容易留下解释空间；\\
- 术语与付款方式、管辖条款分离，可能割裂风险控制；\\
- 别轻信"这个术语对出口商最有利"的笼统说法，要结合具体交易看哪方更能控制风险；\\
- 涉及外国法或贸易惯例细节时，以当地法律与专业意见为准。

# INCOTERMS 2020: Choosing the Right Term

You have been exporting for years, but the FOB, CIF, or DDP line in your contracts is often copied from a buyer's template—and you are not fully sure what it really means. When goods arrive water-damaged and the buyer claims against you, you realise "who bears the risk" was never made clear; when freight spikes or customs stalls, you end up footing the bill. INCOTERMS 2020 is not a game of legal jargon. The term you pick decides the exact moment risk transfers, who pays freight and insurance, and who clears customs. Choose the wrong letter and you may face an uncollectable payment or a large claim. Getting it right saves far more than fixing it afterwards.

## Step 1: grasp the core—risk, cost, obligations

Every trade term answers three questions: **when risk transfers**, **who bears costs**, and **who performs obligations**. To judge whether a term fits you, do not just look at "where delivery occurs"—ask where the risk point lies and whether freight or insurance is treated as implicitly yours. This is the foundation of choosing any term.

## Step 2: know the difference between common terms

- **EXW (Ex Works)**: risk transfers at the factory; the buyer arranges all transport, giving the seller minimal responsibility but also weak retention over title and payment; \\
- **FOB (Free On Board)**: the seller loads the goods and bears cost and risk until loading; freight is the buyer's; \\
- **CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight)**: the seller pays freight and arranges insurance to an agreed extent; risk shifts to the buyer once goods are on board at the port of shipment; \\
- **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)**: the seller bears all freight, insurance, customs clearance and import duties until delivery at destination—the heaviest responsibility; \\
- On specifics such as who clears customs, whose policy covers the goods, and from which port risk shifts, rely on the INCOTERMS 2020 text and the contract.

## Step 3: choose terms with collection in mind

- If you mainly worry about **non-payment or losing control of goods**, pick a term where risk and title pass early, and keep evidence (bills of lading, packing lists, customs documents); \\
- If you worry about **cargo damage claims**, make the risk-transfer point and insurance responsibility clear rather than leaving damage risk with yourself; \\
- For large orders, arrange the payment method (such as letter of credit or partial prepayment) together with the term, to avoid "goods shipped, payment not collected"; \\
- The best term is not the one that looks most seller-friendly; it is the one under which the party best able to control the risk actually does so.

## Time and cost expectations

| Stage | Impact | Note |
|-------|--------|------|
| Term selection | Immediate | Fixes risk, freight, insurance, customs responsibility |
| Contract negotiation | Days to weeks | Settle with payment method and jurisdiction clauses |
| Performance and payment | Runs with the transaction | Term affects liability assessment during collection |

These are experience ranges; rely on the contract and the official INCOTERMS 2020 text. **We do not promise results**, but the right term and complete documents clarify responsibility during recovery.

## Key risks

- Writing "FOB/CIF" without specifying port and risk-transfer point leaves room for dispute; \\
- Separating the term from payment method and jurisdiction clauses can fragment risk control; \\
- Be wary of blanket claims that "this term is best for exporters"; consider who can actually control the risk in your deal; \\
- For foreign law or trade-practice details, follow local law and professional advice.
