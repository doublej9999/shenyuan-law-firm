# 继承法 vs 遗产所在地法：跨境继承适用哪国法律？

父母在中国居住，却在海外留下账户或房产；继承人拿着一份中国公证材料去外国银行办理手续，常会被要求补交当地 probate、法院命令或税务文件。若一开始选错法律路径，可能重复认证、延误出售，甚至错过申报或异议期限。跨境继承没有一条对所有资产都适用的单一规则，必须先拆分问题。

## 第一步：绘制家庭、财产与文件地图

列出被继承人的国籍、死亡时住所或惯常居所、最后居住地，以及每名继承人的所在地。再按国家记录房产、公司股权、银行账户、证券、保险和动产，并标注登记人、受益人及是否有抵押。收集死亡证明、亲属关系证明、遗嘱、婚姻文件和既往赠与记录。中国法与外国法的连接点可能不同，不能只凭国籍判断。

## 第二步：区分实体继承与程序事项

适用法可能分别决定继承人范围、份额、遗嘱效力和保留份；遗产所在地法或登记地规则则常影响房产过户、公司股东变更、银行放款和法院程序。遗嘱形式还可能适用订立地或特定冲突规范。向当地律师核对法院是否承认外国遗嘱、是否需要 probate、认证或附加证明书，以当地法律及专业意见为准。

## 第三步：审查婚姻财产、税费与债务

先从遗产总额中区分夫妻共同财产和被继承人的个人份额，再核对债务、抵押、遗产税、继承税、资本利得税和汇款限制。不同国家可能要求在很短期限内申报。税务处理不能用继承结论替代，涉及中国境内资产和外国资产时，应分别取得专业意见并保存估值依据。

## 第四步：选择办理顺序并留证

通常先确定有权处理遗产的人，再分别向资产所在地提交文件；但若资产有冻结、争议或出售时限，顺序可能相反。保留原件、翻译件、认证链、提交回执和沟通记录。不要先转移或出售存在争议的资产，也不要把中国法院文件当然视为外国机关会直接执行。**不承诺结果**。

# Which Law Applies? Inheritance Conflicts of Law

A parent may have lived in China while leaving a bank account or property overseas. A Chinese notarized document may not be enough for a foreign bank or land registry, which may require probate, a court order, authentication, or tax clearance. Choosing the wrong route can cause repeated filings and missed deadlines. Cross-border inheritance must be analyzed asset by asset.

## Step 1: Map the family, assets, and documents

Record nationality, domicile or habitual residence at death, last residence, and each heir's location. List property, shares, accounts, securities, insurance, and movable assets by country, including registered owner, beneficiary, and security interests. Collect the death certificate, family records, will, marriage documents, and gift records. Nationality alone may not determine the applicable law.

## Step 2: Separate substantive and procedural questions

One law may govern heirs, shares, reserved portions, and will validity, while the law of the asset location governs registration, transfer, bank release, and court procedure. Will formalities may have their own connecting factors. Ask local counsel whether the will is recognized and whether probate, authentication, or an apostille is required.

## Step 3: Review marital property, tax, and debt

Separate community or marital property from the deceased's estate. Check debts, mortgages, estate or inheritance tax, capital gains, and transfer controls. Filing deadlines can be short. Tax analysis is separate from the inheritance result, and valuations should be documented for each jurisdiction. **We do not promise results**.

## Step 4: Choose the order of proceedings

Usually identify the person authorized to administer the estate before approaching each asset registry, but a freeze, dispute, or sale deadline may change the order. Keep originals, translations, authentication records, receipts, and correspondence. Do not sell or transfer disputed assets or assume a PRC order will be directly enforced abroad.
