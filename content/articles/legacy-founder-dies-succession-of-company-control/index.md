# 创始人去世，公司股权由谁接管？

创始人突然去世，家属以为“股份自然由子女继承”，但公司银行账户可能仍由原授权人控制，其他股东也可能依据章程行使优先购买权。股东名册没有更新、遗嘱没有认证或继承人无法证明身份，都会让分红、签字和重大交易停摆。拖延还可能造成客户流失、债务违约和公司资产被不当处分。

## 第一步：先区分股权、职务与经营控制

收集死亡证明、公司章程、股东协议、股权证书、股东名册、董事会决议和遗嘱。股权是遗产的一部分，但董事、法定代表人或银行授权人的职务不一定自动由继承人接替。先确认公司注册地、资产所在地和实际经营地，分别核对中国法律与外国法。

## 第二步：确认继承人和遗嘱效力

在遗产所在地办理继承或遗嘱认证，取得可用于公司登记的证明。存在多名继承人时，应书面确认股份由谁持有、是否出售或共同持有。海外文件通常还涉及公证、认证或附加证明书，具体以当地程序为准。未经确认，不要仅凭家庭协议处分公司股份。

## 第三步：按章程办理控制权变更

通知公司及其他股东，核查转让限制、优先购买权、董事提名权和死锁条款。通过股东会或董事会补选管理人员，更新股东名册、注册信息、银行授权和受益所有人资料。涉及跨境公司时，建议由当地律师核查登记机关要求。

## 时间与费用预期（经验区间）

| 环节 | 经验周期 | 主要成本 |
|---|---|---|
| 文件与章程审查 | 1–3周 | 律师审查费 |
| 继承/遗嘱认证 | 通常1–6个月 | 法院、认证及律师费 |
| 股权与董事变更 | 2–8周 | 登记及法律服务费 |
| 争议处理 | 通常6–24个月 | 律师费、诉讼费与鉴定费 |

以上是经验区间。我们不承诺结果，实际取决于法域、文件完整度和继承人是否有争议。

## 风险提示

不要私自转走公司资金、伪造签字或跳过其他股东权利；不要把遗嘱效力当作当然有效；跨境继承应以当地法律/专业意见为准。

# Founder Dies: Succession of Company Control

When a founder dies, relatives may assume that the shares automatically pass to the children. Yet the company bank account may remain controlled by the former signatory, while co-shareholders may have a right of first refusal. An outdated register, an uncertified will, or unclear heir status can block dividends, signatures, and major transactions. Delay may also cause customer loss, defaults, or misuse of company assets.

## Step 1: Separate shares, offices, and control

Collect the death certificate, articles, shareholders’ agreement, share certificates, register, board resolutions, and will. Shares may form part of the estate, but a director, legal representative, or bank signatory does not necessarily pass to an heir. Identify the company, asset, and operating jurisdictions and check China and foreign law separately.

## Step 2: Confirm heirs and the will

Obtain an inheritance or probate document usable for corporate registration. Where several heirs exist, document who will hold, sell, or jointly own the shares. Foreign documents may require notarization, legalization, or an apostille. Do not dispose of shares on a family agreement alone.

## Step 3: Follow the company’s control process

Notify the company and co-shareholders. Check transfer restrictions, pre-emption rights, director appointment rights, and deadlock clauses. Elect managers, update the register and corporate filings, and replace bank and beneficial-owner authorizations. Local counsel should confirm registry requirements.

## Time and cost expectations (experience ranges)

| Stage | Typical range | Main costs |
|---|---|---|
| Document and articles review | 1–3 weeks | Legal review |
| Inheritance or probate | Usually 1–6 months | Court, certification, and counsel fees |
| Share and director updates | 2–8 weeks | Registry and legal fees |
| Dispute resolution | Usually 6–24 months | Counsel, court, and expert costs |

These are experience ranges. We do not promise results; timing depends on jurisdiction, documents, and disputes.

## Key risks

Do not transfer company funds, forge signatures, or bypass co-shareholder rights. Do not assume a will is automatically valid. Follow local law and professional advice for cross-border estates.
