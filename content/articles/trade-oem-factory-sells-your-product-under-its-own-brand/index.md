# OEM/ODM工厂偷偷贴牌卖你的产品怎么办？

企业投入设计、打样和市场推广后，发现代工厂用相同结构、图片甚至包装向其他买家供货。若先在社交媒体公开指控，可能泄露更多商业秘密，或因证据不足承担名誉和合同风险。处理重点不是一句“这是我的产品”，而是证明权利来源、约定范围、实际销售和造成的损害。

## 第一步：立即固定权利来源

保存设计稿、版本记录、打样邮件、付款凭证、模具清单、商标注册证、专利文件、源文件和交付时间线。核对合同中知识产权归属、保密、禁止销售、模具所有权、区域和客户限制。中国登记权利与外国市场权利要分开审查，不能当然互相替代。

## 第二步：确认是否真的构成违约或侵权

购买并保存疑似产品，记录网址、店铺主体、价格、批次和物流信息，必要时通过合规方式公证网页。比较技术特征、外观、商标和宣传素材，确认工厂是否使用你的资料以及是否存在独立开发或授权。平台投诉、海关备案和法院程序各有证据标准，应先做法域评估。

## 第三步：采取分级止损措施

根据库存、销售速度和证据强度选择律师函、平台下架、海关知识产权保护、合同索赔或临时禁令。要求对方保存生产和销售记录，不要诱导其销毁文件。跨境禁令和保全通常需要证明权利、紧迫性、损害及担保；外国程序以当地律师意见为准。

## 第四步：计算可主张的损失

区分已付款、模具和开发费用、利润损失、合理许可费、库存损失和维权成本，避免把销售额直接等同于损害。检查合同中的责任上限、仲裁条款、法律适用和时效。若继续合作，补签书面授权和审计机制，不要仅凭口头保证复工。**不承诺结果**。

# OEM Factory Sells Your Product Under Its Own Brand

After paying for design, tooling, and samples, a business may discover that its OEM factory is selling a similar product to other buyers under new packaging or branding. A public accusation made too early may expose trade secrets and create contract or defamation risk. The case depends on proving ownership, contractual limits, actual sales, and loss.

## Step 1: Preserve the source of your rights

Keep design files, version history, sample emails, invoices, tooling records, trademark and patent registrations, source files, and delivery timelines. Review ownership, confidentiality, non-sale, tooling, territory, and customer restrictions. PRC registrations and foreign-market rights must be analyzed separately.

## Step 2: Test breach and infringement

Purchase and preserve the suspected product. Record the seller, URL, price, batch, and logistics through lawful means; notarize web evidence where appropriate. Compare technical features, appearance, marks, and marketing material. Platform complaints, customs measures, and court proceedings have different proof requirements.

## Step 3: Use proportionate protective measures

Depending on urgency and evidence, consider a demand letter, platform removal, customs recordal, contractual claim, or interim injunction. Request preservation of production and sales records. Foreign injunctions and preservation orders may require proof of rights, urgency, harm, and security. Obtain local advice.

## Step 4: Quantify the claim

Separate development and tooling costs, lost profit, reasonable royalty, inventory loss, and enforcement costs. Do not equate total sales with damages. Check liability caps, arbitration, governing law, and limitation periods. If cooperation continues, use written authorization and audit rights. **We do not promise results**.
