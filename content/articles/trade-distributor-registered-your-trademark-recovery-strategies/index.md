# 被经销商抢注商标、抢走客户怎么办？

企业进入海外市场后，才发现经销商抢先注册品牌商标，并以自己是“权利人”为由要求平台下架、阻止新经销商接单。若继续争吵而不核对注册状态和合同，可能错过异议期限；若立即撕破合作，又可能触发库存、客户数据和独家区域争议。应先保全证据，再同步处理商标与合同两条线。

## 第一步：核对商标和合同文件

取得官方商标档案、申请日、类别、权利人、转让记录和审查状态，确认是否已注册、是否存在在先申请或第三方权利。逐条检查经销协议中的商标授权、注册义务、禁止申请、终止、区域、客户归属和争议解决条款。商标保护以注册地法为基础，中国权利不能自动覆盖外国市场。

## 第二步：固定使用和合作证据

整理品牌首次使用时间、包装、广告、展会资料、订单、发票、物流、客户邮件和经销商的授权沟通。保存平台通知、下架记录及损失数据。网页和电子文件要记录来源、时间和完整上下文，并按当地程序考虑公证或认证。不要登录对方账户、购买非法客户名单或删除共同业务资料。

## 第三步：选择异议、撤销或转让路径

根据申请阶段和法域规则，评估异议、无效、撤销、不正当竞争或协商转让。若经销商依合同负有代为注册或不得抢注的义务，可同时提出违约请求。海外程序的代理资格、证据翻译、期限和费用不同，应由当地商标律师确认。临时禁令也可能要求担保，不能把申请成功视为当然结果。

## 第四步：保护客户与替代渠道

向重要客户发送克制、可核实的通知，说明授权范围和继续供货安排，避免未经证实的侵权指控。检查客户数据的收集授权、隐私和跨境传输规则。对库存、域名、社交账号和新供应商制定交接方案，并保留谈判记录。**不承诺结果**。

## 风险提示

- 不要错过商标异议、续展或撤销期限。
- 不要把经销授权误当成商标所有权，也不要忽略第三方在先权利。
- 不要非法获取客户资料或公开未经核实的指控。

# Distributor Registered Your Trademark: Recovery Strategies

A distributor may register your brand locally and then ask platforms to remove your listings or block a replacement distributor. Arguing first and checking later can mean missing an opposition deadline. Ending the relationship without review can also trigger disputes over inventory, customer data, and exclusivity. Preserve evidence and address trademark and contract rights together.

## Step 1: Check the trademark and agreement

Obtain the official file, filing date, classes, owner, assignments, and status. Review trademark authorization, registration duties, non-registration promises, termination, territory, customer ownership, and dispute clauses. Trademark protection is territorial; PRC rights do not automatically cover foreign markets.

## Step 2: Preserve use and cooperation evidence

Collect first use, packaging, advertising, trade-show material, orders, invoices, logistics, customer emails, and authorization communications. Preserve platform notices, removals, and loss data with source and time. Use lawful evidence methods and consider notarization. Do not access accounts without permission or buy unlawful customer lists.

## Step 3: Assess opposition, cancellation, or transfer

Depending on status and local rules, consider opposition, invalidation, cancellation, unfair competition, or negotiated transfer. If the distributor breached a duty not to register or to register for you, a contract claim may proceed in parallel. Check local representation, translation, deadlines, and security requirements. **We do not promise results**.

## Step 4: Protect customers and channels

Send important customers a measured, verifiable notice about authorization and supply continuity. Check privacy and cross-border data rules before moving customer information. Plan inventory, domains, social accounts, and replacement channels while preserving negotiation records.
