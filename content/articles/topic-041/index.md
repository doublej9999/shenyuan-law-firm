# 继承时效

"反正那套房子迟早是我的"——这句话在继承里最危险。遗产分配看似天然属于继承人，可法律却给了它一串"保质期"：继承纠纷要主张权利、撤销遗嘱、追回被侵占的遗产，都有诉讼时效与主张期限。等你发现事情不对时，可能已经"过期作废"。跨境继承更复杂：父母在中国、子女在海外，房产在两地，遗嘱认证在普通法国家，各法域的时效长短和起算点完全不同。你每拖延一天，都可能让本可主张的权利永久落空。理解继承时效，是你守住权益的第一步。

## 第一步：分清"继承时效"到底管哪几件事

- **继承权纠纷的诉讼时效**：如继承人之间的继承权受侵害，主张权利有法定时效，期间自你"知道或应当知道权利被侵害"时起算；\
- **遗嘱有效性的挑战期限**：许多普通法地区对"质疑遗嘱"设有较短的期限，过期通常很难再推翻；\
- **追回被侵占/挪用的遗产**：对非法占有的遗产主张返还，同样受时效约束；\
- **主张与申报期限**：遗嘱认证、遗产管理人程序中对债权和继承身份的申报往往设有明确截止日。

## 第二步：确认你案子的"起算点"与"法域"

- 时效起算并非统一，可能自权利被侵害、知道债务人/占有人、或继承人知悉遗嘱存在时开始，务必以具体事实为准；\
- 跨境案件首先确定"适用哪国法律"——是遗产所在地法、继承人经常居所地法、还是遗嘱指定的准据法，直接决定时效长短；\
- 中国内地继承纠纷的诉讼时效规则与普通法地区"挑战遗嘱"的期限不同，不能拿一个地区的规定套用到另一个。

## 第三步：尽早固定证据与主张

- 书面记录你何时知道权利被侵害、何时提出异议，保存邮件、通知与聊天记录；\
- 通过律师发出正式的书面主张（异议函、催告、挑战通知），许多法域以"提出主张"中断或中止时效；\
- 涉及遗嘱认证的，务必按程序在期限内申报身份与异议，错过往往无法补救。

## 第四步：在关键节点前咨询专业律师

时效是否届满、能否中止或中断、如何主张，都高度依赖具体事实与当地法律。建议你在"知悉争议"时而非"决定撕破脸"时才找律师，让律师在窗口期之内帮你设计主张策略与证据方案。

# Inheritance Limitation Periods: Don't Miss the Deadline

"That house will be mine sooner or later" is the most dangerous sentence in an inheritance. Assets may feel like they naturally belong to the heir, but the law puts a shelf-life on them: claims in inheritance disputes, challenges to a will, and recovery of misappropriated estate property are all subject to limitation. By the time you notice something is wrong, it may already be too late. Cross-border inheritance is worse—parents in China, children overseas, property in both places, probate in a common-law country, with each jurisdiction setting different time limits and starting points. Every day you delay can permanently extinguish a claimable right. Understanding these deadlines is your first step to protecting what is yours.

## Step 1: what "inheritance limitation" actually governs

- **Limitation on inheritance-rights disputes**: claims that your inheritance right has been infringed are subject to a statutory period, usually running from when you knew or should have known of the infringement;\
- **Deadlines for challenging a will**: many common-law jurisdictions set short periods to contest a will; after that it is very hard to overturn;\
- **Recovering misappropriated estate assets**: returning claims over unlawfully held estate property are also time-limited;\
- **Claim and notice windows**: probate and estate-administration regimes set clear deadlines for claiming debts and declaring heir status.

## Step 2: nail down the starting point and the governing law

- The clock does not always start the same way—it may run from the infringement, from when you learned of the debtor/holder, or from when the heir learned of the will; facts matter;\
- In cross-border cases, first determine which law applies—the law of the asset's location, the heir's habitual residence, or the law chosen by the will—because that decides the length of the period;\
- Mainland China's rules on inheritance disputes differ from common-law "will challenge" windows; do not transplant one jurisdiction's rules onto another.

## Step 3: fix your evidence and state your claim early

- Record in writing when you learned of the infringement and when you objected; keep emails, notices, and chat records;\
- Issue a formal written claim through counsel (objection, demand, challenge notice); many jurisdictions stop or suspend the clock when a claim is raised;\
- In probate, declare your status and objection within the deadline—missing it is usually unfixable.

## Step 4: consult a lawyer before the key deadlines

Whether time has expired, can be suspended or interrupted, and how to claim depends on facts and local law. See a lawyer when a dispute appears, not only when you are ready for confrontation—let counsel design the claim strategy and evidence plan inside the window.

## Key risks

- Never assume "it is family, there is no rush"—rights do not renew simply because of kinship;\
- Do not miss probate and claim deadlines; once past, they are rarely cured;\
- China law and foreign law diverge on inheritance limitation—follow local law and professional advice when unsure;\
- Beware casual reassurance that "there is plenty of time, no need to spend on advice."
