# 申请破产清算 vs 追收：债权人如何选择？

客户已经停止付款，账户里看不到资金，负责人却说“正在融资”。此时直接起诉可能得到一纸难以执行的判决；贸然申请破产也可能因资产不足而增加成本。真正要解决的问题是：债务人还有哪些资产、是否存在转移迹象、其他债权人是否已经行动，以及哪条路径能保住受偿位置。

## 第一步：核对债权和到期事实

整理合同、发票、交付记录、对账单、付款承诺和担保文件，确认金额、到期日、利息及管辖条款。检查债务人是否承认债务、是否已进入重组或破产程序。时效和申报期限可能不同，不能只等待对方再次承诺。

## 第二步：做资产与优先权评估

调查不动产、股权、应收账款、存货和担保物，并核查是否存在抵押、质押或其他优先债权。若发现转移资产、关联交易或虚假处置，应及时保存证据并咨询当地律师。资产调查必须使用合法渠道，不要购买来源不明的个人信息。

## 第三步：选择组合方案

有可执行资产且证据充分，可先发律师函、谈判或起诉并申请保全；多个债权人争夺有限资产时，应评估破产申报和债权人会议策略；债务人仍有经营价值时，可考虑附担保的分期和解。破产申请、个别执行和撤销权规则以适用法为准。

## 时间与费用预期（经验区间）

| 路径 | 经验周期 | 主要成本 |
|---|---|---|
| 资产与证据评估 | 数天至3周 | 调查与法律评估费 |
| 催收/和解 | 2–8周 | 律师函和谈判费 |
| 诉讼及保全 | 通常3–18个月 | 律师费、诉讼及保全费 |
| 破产申报与分配 | 通常6个月至数年 | 申报、律师及管理人相关费用 |

以上为经验区间。我们不承诺结果，受偿金额取决于资产、优先权和法域。

## 风险提示

不要因债务人“马上融资”而放弃时效或保全；不要重复申报同一债权；破产程序中的普通债权通常不当然优先。中国法律与外国破产法差异明显，拿不准时以当地法律/专业意见为准。

# Bankruptcy vs Collection: Creditor's Choice

A customer has stopped paying while claiming that financing is coming. A lawsuit may produce an unenforceable judgment, while an impulsive insolvency filing may add cost without improving recovery. The real questions are what assets remain, whether they are being moved, whether other creditors have acted, and which route protects your position.

## Step 1: Verify the debt

Organize the contract, invoices, delivery records, statements, payment promises, and security documents. Confirm the amount, due date, interest, and forum clause. Check for an admission, restructuring, or insolvency filing. Limitation and proof-of-claim deadlines may differ.

## Step 2: Assess assets and priority

Review real estate, shares, receivables, inventory, and collateral, together with mortgages, pledges, and priority claims. Preserve evidence of transfers or related-party transactions. Use lawful sources only and do not buy questionable personal data.

## Step 3: Choose a combined path

Where evidence and assets are strong, consider a demand, settlement, or proceedings with preservation. Where creditors compete for limited assets, assess an insolvency claim and creditor-meeting strategy. A secured instalment settlement may suit a debtor with a viable business. Local insolvency law controls.

## Time and cost expectations (experience ranges)

| Route | Typical range | Main costs |
|---|---|---|
| Asset and evidence review | Days to 3 weeks | Investigation and legal review |
| Demand/settlement | 2–8 weeks | Counsel and negotiation |
| Proceedings and preservation | Usually 3–18 months | Counsel, court, and preservation |
| Insolvency and distribution | Usually 6 months to years | Filing, counsel, and administrator costs |

These are experience ranges. We do not promise results; recovery depends on assets, priority, and jurisdiction.

## Key risks

Do not let a promise of financing cause you to miss a deadline. Do not duplicate claims. Ordinary unsecured claims are not automatically preferred. Follow local law and professional advice.
