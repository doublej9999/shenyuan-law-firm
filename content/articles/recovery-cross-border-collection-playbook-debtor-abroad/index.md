# 老赖在国外怎么办：跨境追收策略总览

你的欠款人把公司迁到境外、人也搬去了国外，电话打不通，国内法院判了也找不到可执行的财产，追收就这么悬着。跨境追收难在"人在境外、资产分散、法域交错"，可它并非无解。关键是别急着东一榔头西一棒，而是按一套总策略走：先摸清债务人身份与资产线索，再决定用仲裁还是诉讼拿一份能在境外执行的裁判依据，配合跨境冻结与证据开示，最后在财产所在地启动执行。下面把这条路完整拆给你看。

## 第一步：摸底——债务人身份与海外资产线索

- 摸清债务人及其关联实体（公司、离岸架构、配偶/近亲属名下资产）的真实身份与注册地；\\
- 通过公开登记、征信、尽调、社交与商业线索，梳理其在境外的银行、房产、股权等财产线索；\\
- 摸底的目的是"知道去哪儿执行"，信息越具体，后续路径越清晰。

## 第二步：拿一份能跨境执行的裁判依据

- 若合同有仲裁条款（尤其约定香港、新加坡或伦敦等常见仲裁地），可向权威仲裁机构申请仲裁，裁决更易依《纽约公约》在众多法域执行；\\
- 若无仲裁条款，可依管辖条款在国内或债务人所在地法院诉讼，再走判决承认与执行；\\
- 拿裁判依据时，就要通盘考虑"这份裁决/判决在债务人财产所在地能否执行"，避免赢了却无法兑现。

## 第三步：用保全与证据开示锁住资产

- 在诉讼/仲裁早期评估申请**冻结令**（freezing order）或财产保全，防止资产再被转移；\\
- 借助法院或仲裁庭的**披露程序**，要求债务人列明其实际控制的境外资产；\\
- 涉及关键事实时，可通过跨境证据开示获取交易记录、账户流水等线索。

## 第四步：在财产所在地启动执行并协同多法域

- 拿到裁判依据后，到债务人或财产所在地申请承认/执行，必要时同步在多个法域行动；\\
- 与当地律师协同，评估执行地法院的实务门槛、被执行资产类型与变现难度；\\
- 执行不是一蹴而就，需要持续跟进资产动向与执行进展。

## 时间与费用预期

| 环节 | 经验周期 | 主要成本 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 资产摸底与信息梳理 | 数周至数月 | 调查/征信费用 |
| 仲裁或诉讼取得裁判 | 通常数月 | 仲裁/诉讼费+律师费 |
| 冻结令/证据开示 | 数天至数月 | 律师费+担保安排 |
| 跨境承认与执行 | 通常数月，视法域 | 执行地律师费+执行费用 |

以上均为经验区间，取决于法域、标的与证据完整度。**我们不承诺结果**，但策略越清晰、财产线索越具体，追收成功率越高。

## 风险提示

- 禁编造"包追回""有内幕渠道"等承诺，追收结果取决于证据与法域实务；\\
- 各国对冻结、判决承认的程序与门槛不同，务必以当地法律与专业意见为准；\\
- 跨境跑动周期长、成本高，先评估预期回报再决定投入；\\
- 证据与文书的公证认证不全，会导致执行受阻，务必提前准备。

# Cross-Border Collection Playbook: Debtor Abroad

Your debtor moved the company offshore, relocated overseas, and stopped answering. Back home, even a court judgment finds no executable assets, so collection hangs in limbo. Cross-border collection is hard because the person is abroad, assets are scattered, and jurisdictions overlap—but it is not hopeless. The key is not to scramble in all directions but to follow a playbook: first map the debtor's identity and asset leads, then obtain an enforceable basis via arbitration or litigation, combine freezing and discovery, and finally enforce where the assets sit. Here is the full path.

## Step 1: map—debtor identity and overseas asset leads

- Identify the real identity and registration of the debtor and affiliated entities (companies, offshore structures, spouse/close relative assets); \\
- Use public registers, credit checks, due diligence, and commercial and social leads to trace overseas bank accounts, property, and equity; \\
- Mapping tells you "where to enforce"; the more specific the leads, the clearer the path.

## Step 2: obtain an enforceable basis

- If the contract has an arbitration clause (especially Hong Kong, Singapore, or London), arbitrate there—awards are more easily enforced under the New York Convention across many jurisdictions; \\
- Without arbitration, litigate in China or the debtor's location and then seek recognition and enforcement of the judgment; \\
- When obtaining the enforceable basis, consider from the start whether it can actually be enforced where the debtor's assets are located.

## Step 3: lock down assets with freezing and discovery

- Evaluate applying for a **freezing order** or preservation early in the proceedings to stop further transfers; \\
- Use the court's or tribunal's **disclosure process** to require the debtor to list actual overseas assets; \\
- Where facts matter, use cross-border discovery to obtain transaction records and account flows.

## Step 4: enforce where assets sit and coordinate across jurisdictions

- With the enforceable basis in hand, apply for recognition/enforcement at the debtor's or property's location, acting in several jurisdictions in parallel if needed; \\
- Work with local counsel to assess practical thresholds, asset types, and realisation difficulty; \\
- Enforcement is not one-shot; keep tracking asset movements and progress.

## Time and cost expectations

| Stage | Typical timeline | Main costs |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| Asset mapping and intelligence | Weeks to months | Investigation/credit-check fees |
| Arbitration or litigation | Usually months | Arbitration/litigation fees + legal fees |
| Freezing order/discovery | Days to months | Legal fees + security arrangements |
| Cross-border recognition and enforcement | Usually months, by jurisdiction | Enforcement counsel + execution costs |

These are experience ranges, depending on jurisdiction, amount, and evidence completeness. **We do not promise results**, but a clearer strategy and more specific asset leads raise the chance of success.

## Key risks

- Never rely on promises of "guaranteed recovery" or "inside channels"; results depend on evidence and local practice; \\
- Freezing and judgment-recognition thresholds vary by country—follow local law and professional advice; \\
- Cross-border runs are long and costly; weigh expected returns before committing; \\
- Incomplete notarisation/authentication of evidence and documents stalls enforcement—prepare early.
