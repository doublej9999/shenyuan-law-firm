# 中国境内资产调查：股权/房产/存款怎么查

债务人嘴上说"没钱"，名下却可能有公司股权、房产、理财。执行法官最常问的一句话是："你能提供财产线索吗？"在债务纠纷中，"查得到资产"往往比"打得赢官司"更关键——没有财产线索，胜诉判决也可能只是一纸空文。

中国境内的资产信息，一部分公开可查，一部分只能通过法定程序查询。以下按"自己能查"和"律师/法院才能查"分类说明。

## 一、公开渠道：自己就能查的

- **企业信息**：国家企业信用信息公示系统（gsxt.gov.cn）可查企业登记、股东、注册资本、行政处罚、经营异常；企查查/天眼查等平台的工商档案可补充对外投资与历史变更；
- **动产抵押**：中国人民银行征信中心动产融资统一登记公示系统可查动产抵押与融资租赁登记；
- **知识产权**：通过中国商标网、专利检索系统查询商标与专利；
- **涉诉与执行**：中国裁判文书网、中国执行信息公开网可查涉诉记录、被执行信息与限制消费名单——被执行记录本身说明其有未了债务，也提示其资产可能已被其他债权人盯上。

## 二、非公开信息：须走法定程序

- **房产**：不动产登记信息不向公众开放，个人无法直接查询。实务中通常由律师持法院调查令查询，或由法院依申请查询；
- **银行存款**：银行账户信息属个人隐私，只能由法院查询、冻结、扣划，或律师凭调查令查询；
- **证券/基金/保险**：同样需通过法院或调查令查询。

## 三、调查的合法边界

- 通过"私家侦探"、购买公民个人信息、社工库、爬取内部数据等途径获取的资产线索，证据可能被排除，还可能构成侵犯公民个人信息等犯罪；
- 合法获得的信息，只能用于诉讼保全或执行目的，注意保护第三方隐私，避免对外传播。

## 时间与费用预期

| 调查方式 | 通常周期 | 主要成本 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 公开渠道自查 | 1–5 个工作日 | 低（平台会员费） |
| 律师尽调报告 | 1–2 周 | 按件计费 |
| 法院调查令查询 | 2–8 周 | 律师费+调查费 |
| 法院依申请查询 | 1–3 个月 | 在执行程序内进行 |

以上为经验区间。我们不承诺结果，但可以告诉您某条线索值不值得查、怎么查才合法有效。

## 风险提示

- 不要把公开信息当全部——很多债务人把资产登记在配偶、亲属或关联公司名下；
- 不要只查一次——资产状态动态变化，查封前后都要复查；
- 发现资产后应尽快申请保全或执行，防止转移；
- 切勿购买或使用非法获取的个人信息。

# Investigating Assets Inside China: Equity, Property, Deposits

The debtor insists he has no money — yet he may own company shares, real estate, and investments. The question every enforcement judge asks first is: "Can you provide leads on his assets?" In debt disputes, locating assets often matters more than winning the case. A victorious judgment without executable property is just paper.

In China, some asset information is publicly available; the rest can only be obtained through statutory procedures. Here is what you can check yourself, and what requires a lawyer or the court.

## Public sources: what you can check yourself

- **Business information**: the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (gsxt.gov.cn) shows registration, shareholders, registered capital, administrative penalties, and operating anomalies. Platforms like Qichacha and Tianyancha add outward investment and historical changes;
- **Movable property**: the PBOC Credit Reference Center's unified movable property registration system lists chattel mortgages and finance leases;
- **Intellectual property**: trademark searches (China Trademark Office) and patent searches are open to the public;
- **Litigation and enforcement**: China Judgments Online and the China Enforcement Information Publicity System show litigation records, enforcement records, and restriction lists. An existing enforcement record means other creditors may already be circling the same assets.

## Non-public information: statutory routes only

- **Real estate**: land registration records are not open to the public. In practice, a lawyer must obtain a court investigation order, or the court queries on application;
- **Bank deposits**: account information is private; only a court may query, freeze, or deduct — or a lawyer with an investigation order;
- **Securities, funds, insurance policies**: likewise require court or investigation-order access.

## The legal boundaries of investigation

- Leads obtained through "private detectives," purchased personal data, hacked databases, or scraped internal records may be excluded as evidence and can constitute crimes (such as infringing citizens' personal information);
- Even lawfully obtained findings should be used only for preservation or enforcement purposes, and third-party privacy must be respected.

## Time and cost expectations

| Method | Typical duration | Main costs |
|--------|------------------|------------|
| Public-source search | 1–5 business days | Low (platform fees) |
| Lawyer's due-diligence report | 1–2 weeks | Per-case fee |
| Court investigation order | 2–8 weeks | Counsel fees + investigation costs |
| Court-ordered query | 1–3 months | Within enforcement procedure |

These are experience ranges. We do not promise outcomes, but we can tell you whether a lead is worth pursuing and how to pursue it lawfully.

## Key risks

- Do not treat public records as the whole picture — many debtors hold assets in a spouse's, relative's, or affiliated company's name;
- Do not search once — asset positions change; re-check before and after any freeze;
- Act quickly once assets are found: apply for preservation or enforcement before they are transferred;
- Never buy or use illegally obtained personal data.
