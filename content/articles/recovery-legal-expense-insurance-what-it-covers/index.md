# 诉讼保险（Legal Expense Insurance）实务

追收欠款，最怕的不是"赢不了"，而是"赢了也亏本"——律师费、仲裁费、保全费、差旅费加起来可能吞掉部分甚至全部债权。诉讼保险（Legal Expense Insurance, LEI）存在的意义，就是把这部分"打官司的成本风险"转移给保险公司。但它不是"买了就随便告"，**保什么、不保什么、何时触发，都需要看明白**。

## 诉讼保险到底保障什么

诉讼保险通常覆盖你作为被保险人在法律程序中的**合理律师费与程序费用**，常见的保障方向包括：

- **合同/欠款追收**：对逾期不付的交易提起催收、仲裁或诉讼的律师与程序费用；
- **劳动/租赁/其他争议**：面向个人的劳动合同、租赁纠纷等（因产品线而异）；
- **败诉方费用**：部分产品覆盖被判令承担对方费用或保全担保的费用。

需要说明的是，各国产品差异很大，**具体的保障范围、额度与除外责任，以保单条款为准**。

## 它不保什么（常见除外）

- **投保前已发生的争议**：绝大多数诉讼保险**不覆盖投保前已知或已出现的纠纷**——这正是它要求"尽早投保"的原因；
- **明显无望/无正当性的案件**：保险公司会评估胜诉概率，无胜诉前景的索赔通常不受理；
- **刑事罚款、故意行为、惩罚性赔偿**：通常不在普通民事 LEI 范围内；
- **超出限额或指定律师范围的部分**：部分产品限定律所或要求事先批准。

## 实务操作要点

**1. 在争议发生前投保。** 诉讼保险的最大价值在于把"未来"的诉讼风险转移出去；争议已经冒头再投保，几乎必然被拒绝承保相关费用。

**2. 认真做承保审查（underwriting）。** 保险公司会评估你的证据强度、债务人与胜诉概率。**材料越扎实，越容易获批额度和条件**——所以完整的合同与往来记录是前提。

**3. 弄清触发与报备流程。** 何时启动（是否需事先获批）、如何委托指定律师、费用如何直付或报销，都要在投保时确认清楚。

**4. 结合其他风险工具使用。** 诉讼保险可与出口信用保险、保理、应收账款管理搭配，构成更完整的"回款—风险"体系。

## 时间与费用预期

| 项目 | 经验区间 | 说明 |
|------|----------|------|
| 承保审查与报价 | 1–4 周 | 视证据与风险复杂度 |
| 保费水平 | 视保额/行业 | 通常为保额的一定比例 |
| 理赔审核周期 | 1–3 个月 | 视程序进展与材料 |

## 风险提示

- **最忌"出事才想起买"**：争议已发生的费用，几乎不可能获保，早买才有效；
- **别误以为"全包"**：除外责任、限额、指定律师、预估胜诉门槛都可能不保，条款要逐条读；
- **别为了获保而夸大或多报**：投保时的不实陈述可能导致拒赔；
- **别忽视各国差异**：LEI 在德国、英国等欧洲市场较成熟，在部分国家/地区产品有限，需结合所在市场评估。

对跨境债权人来说，诉讼保险是一种**"以小博大"的成本风险转移工具**，但有效性高度依赖投保时机、核保审查与条款理解。把它放进你的回款风险管理工具箱，远比临时抱佛脚更从容。

# Legal Expense Insurance: What It Covers

When collecting a debt, the real fear is not losing — it is "winning and still losing money", because legal fees, arbitration costs, preservation fees and travel can swallow part or all of the claim. Legal Expense Insurance (LEI) exists to shift this "cost of litigating" risk to an insurer. But it is not "buy it and sue freely" — **what it covers, what it excludes, and when it is triggered all need to be understood.**

## What legal expense insurance actually covers

LEI typically covers the **reasonable lawyer fees and procedure costs** you incur as the insured in legal proceedings. Common directions include:

- **Contract / debt collection**: costs of pursuing collection, arbitration or litigation against a non-paying counterparty;
- **Employment / tenancy / other disputes**: personal employment and tenancy disputes, depending on the product line;
- **Losing-party costs**: some policies cover costs you are ordered to pay or security for costs.

Products differ greatly across countries, and **the exact scope, limits and exclusions always follow the policy wording.**

## Common exclusions

- **Disputes arising before cover starts**: almost all LEI excludes **losses known or existing before you took out the policy** — the very reason it rewards buying early;
- **Frivolous or hopeless claims**: insurers assess chances of success; claims with no reasonable prospect are not accepted;
- **Criminal fines, intentional acts, punitive damages**: usually outside ordinary civil LEI;
- **Amounts beyond limits or outside the designated panel**: some products restrict your choice of counsel or require advance approval.

## Practical points

**1. Buy before the dispute arises.** The core value of LEI is shifting *future* litigation risk away; once a dispute has surfaced, cover for its costs will almost always be refused.

**2. Take underwriting seriously.** Insurers assess the strength of your evidence, the debtor, and the odds of success. **Solid materials win better limits and terms** — complete contracts and correspondence are a prerequisite.

**3. Clarify triggers and reporting.** When to trigger (advance approval?), how to appoint panel counsel, and how fees are paid or reimbursed must be confirmed at placement.

**4. Use it alongside other tools.** Pair LEI with export credit insurance, factoring and receivables management for a fuller collection-risk framework.

## Risk notes

- **The worst move is buying only when trouble hits**: costs for an existing dispute are virtually uninsurable — early purchase is what works;
- **Do not assume it is "comprehensive"**: exclusions, limits, panel counsel and a merits threshold may all apply — read every clause;
- **Do not exaggerate to get covered**: material misstatements at application can void a claim;
- **Do not ignore market differences**: LEI is well developed in Germany, the UK and parts of Europe but limited elsewhere — assess your market.

For cross-border creditors, LEI is a **"small outlay, big risk-transfer" tool**, but its value hinges on timing, honest underwriting and clause comprehension. Making it part of your receivables risk toolkit is far calmer than scrambling at the last minute.
