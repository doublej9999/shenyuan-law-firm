# 公司高管挪用资金、关联交易侵占：股东如何追责？

你持股的那家公司，账面利润一路下滑，可高管名下的车、房、连带公司却越换越高级。查过合同后你才发现：采购价明显高于市价，供应商竟是高管亲戚的公司——钱就是这样被"搬"走的。你一旦犹豫，账目可能被做平、凭证被销毁、关联方把资产转移，追诉时效和证据窗口都会一起溜走。别等年报才察觉，下面把股东可行的追责路径一步步讲清楚。

## 第一步：先行使知情权，把账查清楚

- 依据公司法及相关司法解释，股东有权查阅、复制公司章程、股东会决议、财务报告，也有权要求查阅会计账簿（含原始凭证，各地实践不一）；
- 书面提出请求（写明目的），公司无理拒绝的，可提起"股东知情权之诉"，法院可责令其提供；
- 关键提醒：查账要尽量快速、在对方未察觉时固定全部材料，避免高管抢先销毁或篡改；中国公司法与境外公司法在查阅权限上存在差异，以当地法律/专业意见为准。

## 第二步：评估是否构成挪用、侵占或利益输送

- **挪用资金/职务侵占**：高管将公司资金归个人使用或占为己有，达到一定金额即可能触犯刑责；
- **关联交易侵害**：与关联方交易未按公允定价、未履行披露与表决程序，损害公司利益；
- **自我交易/竞业**：未经允许利用公司机会或资源为自己谋利；
- 这一步需要对照合同、付款流水、关联方工商信息逐笔核对，形成"资金流向图"，是后续追责的证据基础。

## 第三步：按公司治理程序提起追责

- 若时间与程序允许，可先通过股东会/董事会决议，要求公司监事会或监事对高管追责；
- 监事会怠于起诉时，小股东可依程序提起**股东代表诉讼（派生诉讼）**，以公司名义向侵权高管索赔；
- 若高管同时涉刑，可向公安机关**报案**（挪用资金、职务侵占、侵犯商业秘密等），刑事程序可与民事追偿并行——建议同步推进，避免顾此失彼。

## 第四步：同步保全与追收资金

- 发现侵占后第一时间申请**财产保全/冻结**，防止高管或关联方转移资产，这是能否实际追回的关键；
- 追收中要锁定责任人名下的股权、房产、账户与关联公司往来款，必要时追加共同责任人；
- 追收的资金最终归于公司，再由股东按股权分配（或依章程约定），并非直接落入股东口袋。

## 时间与费用预期

| 路径 | 经验周期 | 主要成本 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 知情权之诉/查账 | 数周至数月 | 律师费+诉讼费 |
| 股东代表诉讼 | 6–18个月不等 | 律师费+诉讼/保全费 |
| 刑事报案 | 立案周期因案而异 | 较低（无律师也可能进行） |
| 保全与追收执行 | 数月至数年，视法域 | 保全担保+执行费 |

以上均为经验区间，实际周期与成本取决于案件复杂度、对方配合度与所在法域。我们不承诺结果，但"能否及时固定证据与保全"往往决定追回成败。

## 风险提示

- 不要只停留在"开会质问"，口头沟通不会留下追诉依据，务必留存书面与证据；
- 不要拖延到账目做平、资产转移或时效届满才行动，越早固定证据越有利；
- 不要忽略重要决策可能与高管无关——注意区分善意经营失误与故意侵占，避免误诉；
- 关联交易涉及中国法律与境外公司法，拿不准的以当地法律/专业意见为准；切勿自行销毁或伪造证据。

# Executive Misappropriation: Shareholder Remedies

The company you hold shares in keeps reporting shrinking profits, yet the executive's cars, homes, and connected companies keep getting upgraded. When you finally review the contracts, you notice purchase prices well above market and a supplier that turns out to be run by the executive's relative—money has been quietly moved out. If you hesitate, the books can be smoothed, records destroyed, and related-party assets transferred before you act, taking the limitation period and the evidence window with them. Do not wait for the annual report. Here is how a shareholder can lawfully take action step by step.

## Step 1: exercise inspection rights and pin down the accounts

- Under company law and related judicial interpretations, shareholders may inspect and copy the articles, board resolutions, and financial reports, and may request access to accounting books (raw vouchers depend on local practice);
- Make a written request (state the purpose); if the company unreasonably refuses, you can bring an "inspection (information rights) claim" and the court may order production;
- Key note: move quickly and fix all materials before the executive notices, to prevent tampering. China's Company Law and overseas corporate laws differ on inspection scope—follow local law/professional advice where unclear.

## Step 2: assess whether this is misappropriation, embezzlement, or self-dealing

- **Misappropriation / embezzlement**: an executive using company funds for personal ends or taking them outright, which above certain amounts can be criminal;
- **Related-party harm**: dealing with connected parties off fair price, without disclosure and approval procedures, to the company's detriment;
- **Self-dealing / competition**: using corporate opportunities or resources for personal gain without authorization;
- Cross-check contracts, payment flows, and the related supplier's registration one by one to build a "money-flow map"—this is the evidentiary foundation for any later claim.

## Step 3: pursue remedies through corporate governance first

- Where time and procedure permit, raise the matter at the shareholders'/board meeting and require the supervisory board (or supervisor) to pursue the executive;
- If the supervisory board fails or refuses to act, minority shareholders may bring a **derivative action** and claim damages in the company's name against the executive;
- If criminal conduct is involved, file a complaint with public security (embezzlement, misappropriation, trade-secret theft, etc.). Criminal and civil tracks can run in parallel—advancing both avoids choosing one at the expense of the other.

## Step 4: preserve assets and recover funds in parallel

- At the first sign of wrongdoing, apply for **asset preservation / freezing** to stop the executive or related parties moving assets—this often decides whether the money is ever recovered;
- Identify the responsible person's equity, property, accounts, and related-company flows, adding joint wrongdoers where needed;
- Recovered funds return to the company and are then distributed to shareholders by ownership (or per the articles); they do not go straight to a shareholder's pocket.

## Time and cost expectations

| Route | Typical timeline | Main costs |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| Inspection / accounts action | Weeks to months | Counsel + court fees |
| Derivative action | 6–18 months, varies | Counsel + litigation/preservation |
| Criminal filing | Depends on the case | Low (possible without counsel) |
| Preservation and enforcement | Months to years, jurisdiction-dependent | Security + enforcement fees |

These are experience ranges; actual timelines and costs depend on complexity, the counterparty's conduct, and the jurisdiction. We do not promise results, but whether evidence and preservation are secured in time often decides the outcome.

## Key risks

- Do not stop at verbal confrontation at a meeting—there is no basis to sue without written records and evidence;
- Do not wait until the books are smoothed, assets moved, or the limitation period lapses; earlier evidence wins;
- Do not assume every bad decision is intentional—distinguish honest business mistakes from deliberate misappropriation to avoid a mistaken claim;
- Related-party matters touch China law and foreign corporate law; follow local law/professional advice where unclear, and never destroy or fabricate evidence.
