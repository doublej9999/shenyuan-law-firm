# 如何查到债务人在海外的资产？

"赢了官司，找不到钱"是跨境执行的头号难题。判决只是起点，**找到可执行资产**才是回款的关键。好消息是：现代商业社会里，大多数资产都会留下公开痕迹。本文介绍海外资产调查的合法途径与实操方法。

## 第一层：公开登记与官方记录（免费/低成本）

- **公司登记**：在美国各州Secretary of State、加拿大公司注册处、香港公司注册处等查询债务人公司信息、股东、董事、注册地址；
- **不动产登记**：许多国家/地区的房产登记可以公开或半公开查询（如美国部分郡县、加拿大部分省份）；
- **法院记录**：诉讼记录、破产记录、判决记录（如美国PACER系统、加拿大部分court records）——债务人的其他债权人和诉讼历史往往暴露资产线索；
- **知识产权登记**：商标、专利、域名WHOIS——经营资产的一部分。

## 第二层：信用与商业信息报告（低成本）

- 商业信用报告（如邓白氏D&B、Equifax商业版、当地信用局）提供企业付款记录、负面事件；
- 银行与支付信息（如果债务人公开经营，其收款账户、公司地址往往可从发票、官网、贸易平台页面提取）。

## 第三层：开源情报（OSINT）

- **公司官网**：地址、办公地点、关联公司、负责人信息；
- **社交媒体**：LinkedIn、Facebook、当地社交平台——职位、办公地、关联企业；
- **新闻与公告**：并购、投资、中标、纠纷报道；
- **地图与街景**：实地经营地点的确认（是否有实际办公场所）；
- **贸易与行业数据库**：参展记录、行业名录、海关数据（部分国家公开）。

OSINT的要点是**交叉验证**：单一信息源不可靠，多个来源互相印证才有价值，也才经得起法庭检验。

## 第四层：委托专业调查（按需）

涉及复杂架构（离岸公司、信托、代持）或债务人刻意隐藏资产时，可委托当地持牌调查机构。调查范围通常包括：

- 银行账户（部分法域可通过法院程序/披露令获得）；
- 房产与车辆登记；
- 关联公司与实际受益人结构；
- 配偶、亲属名下的可疑资产。

**合规红线**：调查必须通过合法渠道，非法侵入、冒充、收买内部人员获取的信息不仅没有证据效力，还可能带来刑事责任。律师主导的调查程序（如证据保全、披露令、冻结令）是更稳妥的路径。

## 查到资产后：立即保全

资产线索的价值在于时效——查到后应立即通过法律程序保全：

- **冻结令（Freezing Order）**：禁止处置资产，防止转移；
- **披露令（Disclosure Order）**：要求债务人披露资产详情；
- **扣押令**：直接控制特定资产。

保全申请通常需要"资产将灭失/转移的紧迫风险"证明，这与调查结果直接相关——调查越扎实，保全越容易获批。

## 给企业主的建议

- 跨境执行前先做一次资产摸底，评估"值得不值得追"；
- 资产调查与保全、执行同步规划，避免"查到但保不住"；
- 涉及离岸架构的，尽早咨询，越复杂越要早启动。

# How to Locate a Debtor's Overseas Assets

"Won the case, cannot find the money" is the number one challenge in cross-border enforcement. A judgment is only the start — **finding executable assets** is what actually gets you paid. The good news: in the modern commercial world, most assets leave public traces. This article covers lawful overseas asset tracing, layer by layer.

## Layer 1: Public registries and official records (free / low cost)

- **Company registers**: search the debtor's corporate records, shareholders, directors, and registered address via US Secretaries of State, Canadian registries, Hong Kong's Companies Registry, and equivalents;
- **Property registries**: many jurisdictions offer open or semi-open real estate searches;
- **Court records**: litigation, bankruptcy, and judgment records (e.g. US PACER) — other creditors and litigation history often expose asset trails;
- **IP registries**: trademarks, patents, and domain WHOIS — part of the operating asset picture.

## Layer 2: Credit and business information reports (low cost)

- Business credit reports (D&B, Equifax business, local bureaus) show payment behavior and negative events;
- Bank and payment details can often be extracted from invoices, websites, and marketplace pages if the debtor operates openly.

## Layer 3: Open-source intelligence (OSINT)

- **Company website**: address, premises, affiliates, key personnel;
- **Social media**: LinkedIn, Facebook, local platforms — positions, offices, related businesses;
- **News and announcements**: M&A, investments, tenders, dispute coverage;
- **Maps and street view**: verify actual operating premises;
- **Trade and industry databases**: exhibitions, directories, customs data where public.

The key to OSINT is **cross-verification**: single sources are unreliable; corroborated findings carry weight — and survive courtroom scrutiny.

## Layer 4: Professional investigation (as needed)

For complex structures (offshore companies, trusts, nominees) or deliberately hidden assets, engage licensed local investigators. Scope typically includes:

- Bank accounts (in some jurisdictions via court process or disclosure orders);
- Property and vehicle registrations;
- Affiliated companies and beneficial ownership structures;
- Suspicious assets held by spouses or relatives.

**Compliance red lines**: investigations must use lawful channels. Information obtained by intrusion, impersonation, or bribing insiders has no evidentiary value and can create criminal exposure. Lawyer-led procedures (discovery, disclosure orders, freezing orders) are the safer route.

## After finding assets: preserve them immediately

The value of an asset lead is its freshness — preserve without delay through legal process:

- **Freezing order**: prohibits disposal pending resolution;
- **Disclosure order**: compels the debtor to reveal asset details;
- **Seizure order**: takes control of specific assets.

Preservation applications usually require proof of a real risk of dissipation — which connects directly to your investigation. The more solid the investigation, the more likely preservation is granted.

## Advice for business owners

- Run an asset reconnaissance before cross-border enforcement to judge whether it is worth pursuing;
- Plan tracing, preservation, and enforcement together — avoid "found but not preserved";
- For offshore structures, consult early — the more complex, the earlier to start.
