# 判决承认与执行的时效和程序要点

胜诉后，债务人却把账户、房产或经营主体放在另一个国家。债权人如果只盯着判决金额，不核对执行地的申请期限、终局性和送达材料，可能在投入翻译认证费用后被驳回。承认与执行不是自动发生的后续动作，而是一套独立的法域和证据审查。

## 第一步：确认判决能否申请

核对作出判决的法院是否有可被执行地接受的管辖基础，判决是否已经终局、可执行，是否仍在上诉或和解审查中。确认被申请人的名称、地址和责任范围完全一致。仲裁裁决应另行检查仲裁协议、仲裁地、裁决书和撤销程序状态。中国判决、外国判决和仲裁裁决适用的规则并不相同。

## 第二步：先查期限再查资产

向执行地律师确认承认与执行的时效起算点、申请方式和是否存在中断或延长规则。同步调查公开登记、诉讼记录、公司信息和合规商业线索，确认资产是否在该地、是否有优先权或已被处置。不能把公开线索当作已确认资产，也不能为了追债非法获取个人金融信息。

## 第三步：准备完整申请包

通常需要认证判决或裁决、终局证明、送达证明、原合同或仲裁协议、利息计算、当事人身份证明和准确翻译。不同法院可能要求附加证明书、公证、宣誓翻译或当地代理。逐项制作文件清单和版本控制，避免姓名、金额、日期在翻译中不一致。

## 第四步：预判抗辩和执行衔接

被执行人可能提出未有效送达、程序不公、无管辖、已履行、重复程序或公共政策抗辩。申请前评估担保、资产保全、冻结令和执行费用，并确认承认裁定作出后如何登记、扣押或变现。承认成功不等于立即收款，资产不足或其他债权人优先时仍可能无法足额回收。**不承诺结果**。

## 风险提示

- 不要假定所有国家都承认外国判决，也不要错过申请时效。
- 不要提交未经认证、翻译不一致或无法证明送达的材料。
- 不要把承认裁定等同于资产已冻结或款项已到账。

# Recognition and Enforcement: Time Limits and Procedure

A winning creditor may still face a debtor whose accounts, property, or business is in another country. If the creditor ignores local deadlines, finality rules, and service documents, an application may fail after substantial translation and filing costs. Recognition and enforcement is a separate jurisdictional and evidentiary process, not an automatic sequel to judgment.

## Step 1: Confirm eligibility

Check the issuing court's jurisdiction, whether the judgment is final and enforceable, and whether an appeal or settlement review remains pending. Match names, addresses, and liability precisely. For an award, separately review the arbitration agreement, seat, award, and annulment status. PRC judgments, foreign judgments, and awards may follow different regimes.

## Step 2: Check deadlines and assets first

Ask counsel in the enforcement forum about the limitation period, starting point, filing route, and tolling rules. Investigate public registries, court records, corporate information, and lawful commercial sources. Confirm whether assets remain there and whether they have priorities or been transferred. Public leads are not confirmed assets, and private financial data must not be obtained unlawfully.

## Step 3: Build the application package

The package may require an authenticated judgment or award, finality certificate, proof of service, contract or arbitration agreement, interest calculation, identity documents, and accurate translations. Apostilles, notarization, sworn translations, or local counsel may be required. Use a controlled checklist so names, dates, and amounts remain consistent.

## Step 4: Anticipate defenses and execution

Defenses may include defective service, unfair procedure, lack of jurisdiction, payment, duplication, or public policy. Assess security, preservation, freezing orders, and costs before filing. Confirm how a recognition order will lead to seizure or realization. Recognition does not guarantee immediate or full recovery. **We do not promise results**.

## Risk warnings

- Do not assume every country recognizes foreign judgments or overlook deadlines.

- Do not equate recognition with a freeze or payment.
