# 房产继承中的夫妻共同财产分割

家人去世后，继承人拿着房产证准备办理过户，却发现房产登记在夫妻一方名下，另一方配偶主张房子有其一半。若先签出售或分割协议，可能导致交易暂停、买方索赔，甚至把本不属于遗产的份额纳入争议。跨境案件还会叠加房屋所在地法、婚姻财产法和继承法，拖延的代价通常是维护费、税费和证据灭失风险。

## 第一步：先锁定适用的财产范围

确认结婚时间、住所、国籍、婚前协议及房产购买时间。中国法与外国法对婚后所得、登记名义和分别财产的规则可能不同，不能仅凭房产证判断。房产所在地的不动产规则通常尤其重要，以当地法律和专业意见为准。

## 第二步：建立证据包

收集产权证、购房合同、付款流水、贷款记录、装修支出、婚姻证明、遗嘱、死亡证明和税费凭证。境外文件按所在地要求办理认证、附加证明书或翻译；保留原件、电子元数据及完整流水，避免只提交截图。

## 第三步：分两层计算

先确定配偶因婚姻财产取得的固有份额，再对 deceased 配偶剩余份额适用继承规则。列出所有继承人、遗嘱效力、债务和可能的特留份；不要把“继承比例”直接套在整套房产上。争议无法协商时，先申请限制处分或查封，再选择调解、诉讼或当地 probate 程序。

## 第四步：选择落地方案

可按份共有、折价补偿、出售分配或设立信托（须符合法域规则）处理。签署协议前核对汇款、税费、外汇及未成年继承人保护要求。境外房产过户应由当地律师和登记机关确认材料清单。

## 时间与费用预期（经验区间）

|事项|经验区间|费用因素|
|---|---:|---|
|材料整理与份额初评|2–6周|翻译、认证、律师费|
|协商与调解|1–4个月|参与人数与法域|
|诉讼或 probate|6–24个月|法院、审级、争议额|
|过户或出售|1–6个月|税费、登记及汇率|

以上均为经验区间，不承诺结果，实际以当地法律、材料完整度和案件复杂程度为准。

## 风险提示

不要擅自出售、出租或转移争议房产；不要隐瞒婚前协议、债务或其他遗嘱；不要把中国继承规则直接套用于外国不动产。签协议前应核实所有权、继承人范围和税务后果。

# Marital vs Separate Property in Estate Division

After a death, heirs may see one spouse's name on a title while the surviving spouse claims an ownership share. Selling first can freeze the transaction, trigger buyer claims, and create a dispute over property that was never part of the estate. Cross-border cases may involve the law of the property location, matrimonial-property rules, and succession law.

## Step 1: Identify the governing property rules

Review marriage date, habitual residence, nationality, any marital agreement, and the acquisition date. A title alone may not answer whether an asset is marital or separate. Immovable-property rules of the location commonly matter; obtain local legal advice.

## Step 2: Build the evidence file

Collect the title, purchase contract, payment and loan records, marriage and death certificates, will, renovation invoices, and tax receipts. Authenticate and translate foreign documents as required. Preserve originals and complete bank records, not screenshots alone.

## Step 3: Calculate in two layers

First identify the surviving spouse's own matrimonial share. Then apply succession rules only to the deceased spouse's remaining share, considering heirs, will validity, debts, and protected shares. If agreement fails, seek interim protection before choosing mediation, litigation, or probate.

## Step 4: Choose an executable outcome

Options include co-ownership, a buyout, sale and distribution, or a locally valid trust. Confirm taxes, registration, foreign-exchange controls, and safeguards for minors before signing.

## Time and cost expectations (experience ranges)

|Task|Typical range|Cost drivers|
|---|---:|---|
|Initial review|2–6 weeks|Translation and counsel|
|Negotiation|1–4 months|Number of heirs and forum|
|Litigation or probate|6–24 months|Court and complexity|
|Transfer or sale|1–6 months|Taxes and registration|

These are experience ranges. No outcome is promised; local law and the evidence control the result.

## Risks

Do not sell, lease, or transfer disputed property without advice. Do not omit agreements, debts, or heirs, or apply Chinese succession rules automatically to foreign real estate.
