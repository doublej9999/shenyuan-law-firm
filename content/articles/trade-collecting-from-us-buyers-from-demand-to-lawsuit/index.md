# 美国客户拖欠货款：从催收到诉讼的全流程

你的货发出去了，美国客户却以"质量有问题""现金流紧张""款还没到"为由一拖再拖。外贸订单金额高、账期长，美国又在另一时区、另一套法律体系里，隔着太平洋追款似乎无从下手。你越是不敢启动正式程序，对方就越认定你"不会来真的"，欠款越拖越久。其实美国欠款追收有清晰的路径：从小额养成书面档案，到发催款函、谈和解与担保，再到必要时走小额诉讼或州法院。把每一步做对，就能把坏账变成可执行的权利。

## 第一步：先固定证据，建立"书面档案"

- 整理销售合同/订单、发票、提运单据、签收记录、对账单与往来邮件，明确付款条件、到期日、适用法律与争议解决条款；\
- 用可追踪方式（邮件+快递）发出书面催款函，写明欠付金额、到期日、利息与最后宽限期；\
- 同步留意诉讼时效（美国各州不同，通常数年，以合同准据法为准），别让时效在拉锯中溜走。

## 第二步：先谈判与担保，尽量不上法庭

- 若客户仍经营、争议仅在金额，可分阶段还款或引入担保，把口头承诺落成书面和解；\
- 让美国本地律师介入以催款函和谈判施压——很多美国客户在收到正式律师函后才会认真对待；\
- 和解协议务必列明违约后果、加速到期条款与法院选择，避免二次拖欠。

## 第三步：选择"打得动"的诉讼路径

- **金额较小**（各州上限不同，通常数千美元）：可走小额诉讼（small claims），程序简、费用低，但多数不允许律师代理、执行渠道有限；\
- **金额较大**：在美国州法院起诉，通常需本地律师配合，主张货款、利息与可能的律师费；\
- 看合同是否有仲裁条款或专属管辖约定，有约定则优先按约定走，别忽略程序前提。

## 第四步：诉讼之后的执行

- 拿到判决后，真正到钱靠"执行"——在美国执行需按债务人资产所在地启动，如工资扣减（garnishment）、银行账户冻结、财产拍卖等；\
- 若债务人有在中国或其他国家的资产，可评估在当地申请承认与执行的可行性。

## 时间与费用预期

| 环节 | 经验周期 | 主要成本 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 证据整理与催款函 | 数天至数周 | 低（咨询/函件费） |
| 谈判与和解 | 数周至数月 | 律师费（可谈固定） |
| 小额诉讼 | 通常数月 | 法院费用+少量律师费 |
| 州法院诉讼至判决 | 通常 6–18 个月 | 律师费+诉讼费 |
| 执行 | 数月，视资产与法域 | 执行律师费+法院费用 |

以上均为经验区间，取决于金额、债务人配合度与所在州。**我们不承诺结果**，但"是否及时书面主张与保全"直接影响能否追回。

## 风险提示

- 别只发口头催款不留书面证据，导致时效与取证被动；\
- 跨境诉讼涉及美国州法、证据规则与中国法律的差异，拿不准的以当地法律/专业意见为准；\
- 谨防"包回款"话术，美国诉讼变量多，应要求律师书面说明路径与费用；\
- 重要订单建议在合同阶段就约定争议解决与成本承担条款，为事后追收留好"后门"。

# Collecting from US Buyers: From Demand to Lawsuit

Your goods shipped, and the US buyer stalls with "quality issues," "tight cash flow," "payment not approved yet." International orders are large with long payment terms, and the buyer sits in another time zone under another legal system—recovering across the Pacific feels impossible. The longer you avoid formal steps, the more the buyer assumes you "will not follow through," and the debt ages. US collection follows a clear path: build a written file, send demand letters, negotiate settlement and guarantees, then if needed pursue small claims or state court. Getting each step right turns a bad debt into an enforceable right.

## Step 1: fix the evidence, build a written file

- Organise the sales contract/PO, invoices, shipping documents, signed receipts, reconciliations, and email threads; note payment terms, due date, governing law, and the dispute clause;\
- Send a formal written demand by trackable means (email plus courier) stating the amount, due date, interest, and a final grace period;\
- Watch the limitation period (varies by US state, usually several years, per contract governing law) so it does not lapse while you negotiate.

## Step 2: negotiate and secure guarantees before any court

- If the buyer still operates and the dispute is about amount, agree staged repayment or a guarantee, reduced to a written settlement;\
- Have local US counsel issue demand letters and apply settlement pressure—many US buyers take matters seriously only after a formal attorney letter;\
- Make the settlement state defaults, an acceleration clause, and chosen forum to prevent a second default.

## Step 3: pick a litigation route you can win

- **Smaller amounts** (state caps differ, often a few thousand dollars): small claims—simple and low cost, but usually no attorney and limited enforcement;\
- **Larger amounts**: sue in US state court, typically with local counsel, claiming the debt, interest, and possibly attorney fees;\
- Check whether the contract has arbitration or an exclusive forum clause; if so, follow it first and do not miss procedural prerequisites.

## Step 4: enforcement after judgment

- A judgment alone is not money—enforcement follows the debtor's assets, via wage garnishment, bank account freeze, or property sale;\
- If the debtor also has assets in China or elsewhere, assess recognition and enforcement locally.

## Time and cost expectations

| Stage | Typical timeline | Main costs |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| Evidence and demand letter | Days to weeks | Low (consult/letter fee) |
| Negotiation and settlement | Weeks to months | Counsel fees (fixed quotes possible) |
| Small claims | Usually months | Filing fees + modest counsel cost |
| State court to judgment | Usually 6–18 months | Counsel + filing fees |
| Enforcement | Months, by assets and state | Enforcement counsel + court costs |

These are experience ranges, depending on amount, cooperation, and state. **We do not promise results**, but whether you claim and preserve in writing early often decides recovery.

## Key risks

- Do not rely only on verbal reminders—you lose leverage and evidence;\
- Cross-border litigation mixes US state law, evidence rules, and Chinese law; when unsure, follow local law and professional advice;\
- Be wary of "guaranteed recovery" pitches; US litigation has many variables—ask for the path and fees in writing;\
- For key orders, address dispute resolution and cost-shifting in your contract so collection has a solid footing later.
