# 事务律师：跨国案件中的角色与如何选择

你跟英国、香港的公司做贸易，货款被拖欠了，对方律师函上的署名是"事务律师"；你想去当地法院申请追收，中介也让你先"找一位事务律师"。可你真到了要动手的时候却一头雾水：事务律师到底帮我办什么？资料交给他安不安全？收费会不会是个无底洞？很多人正是在"该找谁、先办手续还是先发催款函"之间来回犹豫，把追收窗口和保全时机关闭前那段路慢慢拖掉。事实上，事务律师通常就是为你全程起草文件、对接法院与对方律师、推进追收执行的那个人——先把他的角色和选人标准搞懂，是你少走弯路、加快拿回欠款的第一步。

## 第一步：弄清事务律师在跨国案件里具体负责什么

- **文书与程序**：草拟催款函、起诉状、执行申请，处理管辖、送达、公证认证等程序性事项；\\
- **对接与推进**：代替你与对方律师、法院、仲裁机构沟通，安排证据、协调证人出庭；\\
- **策略建议**：评估案件事实与法律依据，建议先协商、催收还是直接进入诉讼/仲裁并提示时效风险；\\
- **联络执行地律师**：在需联合当地律师时组织分工，牵头追收与判决/裁决的跨境执行。

## 第二步：如何筛选一位靠谱的事务律师

- **核实执业资质**：确认对方在对应法域合法执业，可通过当地律师协会公开名录核验；\\
- **看同类案件经验**：问清是否处理过跨境欠款追收与执行，而非只做过本地普通案件；\\
- **要书面方案与报价**：请对方写明可选路径、预估时间与费用区间、由谁负责哪一步；\\
- **约定汇报机制**：要求定期书面进度更新，避免在语言与法域差异中"失联"。

## 第三步：委托时的费用与协作要点

- **问清收费方式**：普通法地区常按小时或分阶段计费，要求书面列明预估区间与可能增加的项目；\\
- **区分事务律师与大律师**：涉及出庭时可能另行延聘律师，两者费用分开计，别把两笔钱混在一起；\\
- **书面确认授权范围**：明确哪些决策需要你同意，避免手续越走越远、费用越叠越高；\\
- **同步核对时效与保全时机**：让事务律师第一时间书面提示诉讼时效与冻结令等保全窗口。

关于事务律师与大律师在出庭职责上的细致分工，可参考我们另一篇《[事務律師 vs 大律師：跨境案件该找谁？](/articles/recovery-what-is-a-solicitor-vs-barrister-cross-border)》。

## 时间与费用预期

| 环节 | 经验周期 | 主要成本 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 律师评估与委托 | 数天至数周 | 前期评估/委托费 |
| 催款与协商阶段 | 数周至数月 | 按小时或分阶段计 |
| 诉讼/仲裁程序 | 通常数月，视法域 | 律师费+法院费用 |
| 判决/裁决跨境执行 | 通常数月，视资产情况 | 执行地律师费+执行费用 |

以上均为经验区间，取决于案件金额、复杂程度与当地费率。**我们不承诺结果**，但提前明确"谁负责、怎么收费"能显著减少无效支出。

## 风险提示

- 不要只看头衔，务必核实执业资质与同类案件经验；\\
- 谨防"包赢、包追回"的承诺，对这类说法保持警惕；\\
- 跨境程序的取证、送达、认证与中国内地差异大，以当地法律与专业意见为准；\\
- 费用务必书面约定区间，防止无底洞式的追加收费。

# Solicitor in Cross-Border Cases: Role and How to Choose

You trade with a UK or Hong Kong company, payment is overdue, and the demand letter is signed by a "solicitor." When you finally want to act, you are unsure what a solicitor actually does, whether it is safe to hand over documents, and whether fees will spiral. Many people hesitate between "whom to instruct" and "send a demand letter or file first," and the collection window slips away. In practice, the solicitor is usually the person who drafts your documents, deals with the court and the opposing lawyer, and drives the recovery forward. Understanding the role and how to vet a good one is your first step to cutting waste and getting paid sooner.

## Step 1: what a solicitor actually handles in a cross-border case

- **Documents and procedure**: drafting demand letters, pleadings, and enforcement applications; handling jurisdiction, service, and notarisation/apostille;\\
- **Liaison and progress**: dealing with opposing counsel, courts, and tribunals on your behalf; organising evidence and arranging witnesses;\\
- **Strategy advice**: assessing the facts and legal basis, and advising whether to negotiate, send a demand letter, or go straight to litigation/arbitration while flagging limitation periods;\\
- **Coordinating local counsel**: dividing work when a local lawyer is needed and leading cross-border enforcement efforts.

## Step 2: how to vet a capable solicitor

- **Verify credentials**: confirm the lawyer is licensed in the relevant jurisdiction, using public rosters of the local bar association;\\
- **Check relevant experience**: ask whether they have handled cross-border debt collection and enforcement, not just routine local work;\\
- **Request a written plan and quote**: ask for the available paths, estimated timeline and fee range, and who handles each step;\\
- **Set a reporting cadence**: require periodic written updates so the matter never goes quiet amid language and legal differences.

## Step 3: fees and how to work together

- **Ask how billing works**: common-law fees are often hourly or staged; request a written range and what could add cost;\\
- **Separate solicitor and barrister fees**: where advocacy is needed, a barrister may be separately engaged and billed;\\
- **Confirm your approval rights**: clarify which decisions need your consent so the scope does not drift;\\
- **Match timing**: have the solicitor promptly flag limitation periods and preservation windows such as freezing orders.

For how solicitors and barristers divide advocacy work, see our related piece, *[Solicitor vs Barrister: Who Handles Your Cross-Border Case?](/articles/recovery-what-is-a-solicitor-vs-barrister-cross-border)*.

## Time and cost expectations

| Stage | Typical timeline | Main costs |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| Evaluation and engagement | Days to weeks | Front-end/evaluation fee |
| Demand and negotiation | Weeks to months | Hourly or staged billing |
| Litigation/arbitration | Usually months, by jurisdiction | Legal fees + court costs |
| Cross-border enforcement | Usually months, depends on assets | Enforcement counsel + execution costs |

These are experience ranges; figures depend on value, complexity, and local rates. **We do not promise results**, but clarifying "who does what and how fees break down" early cuts waste.

## Key risks

- Do not rely on titles alone—verify license and relevant experience;\\
- Be wary of "guaranteed win" or "guaranteed recovery" promises;\\
- Evidence, service, and notarisation differ sharply from mainland practice—follow local law and professional advice;\\
- Agree fees in writing to avoid escalating, unbounded charges.
