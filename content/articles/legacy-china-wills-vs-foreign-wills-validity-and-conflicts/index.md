# 中国立遗嘱 vs 海外立遗嘱：效力与冲突

你在中国和海外都有资产，也想提前把传承安排清楚，可到底该在中国立遗嘱还是在海外立？在中国公证处立的遗嘱，到了国外、尤其普通法国家，法院认不认？反过来，你在海外请律师立的遗嘱，回国后国内机构会不会以"不符合中国法定形式"为由不予认可？很多人的资产分散两地，却只有一份遗嘱，结果在继承时发现效力存疑甚至冲突。搞清楚两边的形式要件与冲突规则，才能避免"遗嘱写得再清楚也用不上"。

## 第一步：看懂两地对遗嘱形式要件的差别

- **中国**：法定遗嘱形式包括自书、代书、打印、录音录像、口头、公证等，各有严格要求（如自书遗嘱须亲笔签名并注明年月日，代书须两名以上见证人等）；\\
- **海外普通法国家**：通常要求书面并签名，部分法域还需见证人签署（often two witnesses），公证并非普遍强制；\\
- 形式是否合规，直接决定遗嘱效力，务必比照遗嘱订立地的法律核验。

## 第二步：理解"承认"与"冲突"的基本规则

- 遗嘱的内容与效力常适用被继承人死亡时经常居所地法，而**不动产一般适用不动产所在地法**；\\
- 因此在中国有效的遗嘱，未必自动在海外对海外房产生效；在中国境外所订遗嘱，海外效力如何认定也以当地法律为准；\\
- 两份遗嘱内容若有冲突，不同法域可能作出不同认定，需专门的冲突法分析。

## 第三步：如何让遗嘱更"稳"

- 若资产分布多地，建议评估是否需要"并行遗嘱"或补充安排，并在遗嘱中明确适用法选择；\\
- 确保每份遗嘱按对应订立地法满足形式要件，必要时由当地律师把关；\\
- 妥善保管并告知可信赖的执行人/继承人遗嘱位置，避免"找不到遗嘱"让继承陷入争产。

## 风险提示

- 别以为"在中国公证过"就通行海内外，海外认定另有一套规则；\\
- 自书遗嘱若缺日期、签名或写得不清楚，极易被挑战；\\
- 遗嘱与婚后财产、子女份额等交织，可能引发效力之外的权利冲突；\\
- 涉及外国继承法与冲突规则时，以当地法律与专业意见为准。

# China Wills vs Foreign Wills: Validity and Conflicts

You hold assets both in China and overseas and want to plan succession clearly—but should you make the will in China or abroad? Will a will notarised in China be accepted by a foreign court, especially in common-law countries? Conversely, will the will you drafted with a lawyer overseas be accepted back home, or rejected for failing to meet Chinese formalities? Many people with assets in two places hold only one will and later find its validity questioned or conflicting. Understanding the formal requirements and conflict rules in both places is the way to avoid a "clear will that cannot be used."

## Step 1: understand the different formal requirements

- **China**: statutory will forms include holographic, notarised, typed, audio-visual, oral, and others, each with strict rules (a holographic will must be personally signed with the date; a will written on behalf must have two or more witnesses); \\
- **Overseas common-law countries**: usually require writing and a signature; some jurisdictions also require signing before witnesses, while notarisation is not always mandatory; \\
- Because formal compliance determines validity, always verify against the law where the will is made.

## Step 2: understand recognition and conflict basics

- Will contents and validity often follow the law of the deceased's habitual residence at death, while **immovables generally follow the lex situs**; \\
- A will valid in China is not automatically effective for overseas property, and how a foreign-made will is treated also depends on local law; \\
- Where two wills conflict, different jurisdictions may reach different results, requiring specific conflicts-of-law analysis.

## Step 3: make the will more solid

- If assets span several places, evaluate whether parallel wills or supplementary arrangements are needed, and state the chosen governing law; \\
- Ensure each will satisfies the formal requirements of its place of making, with local counsel reviewing where appropriate; \\
- Store it safely and tell a trusted executor/heir where it is, to avoid "the will cannot be found" triggering estate disputes.
