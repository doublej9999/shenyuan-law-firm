# 伪造公章、伪造签名的合同效力问题

对方突然拿出一份带有公司印章的合同，要求付款或主张违约；公司内部却没人承认签署。贸然撕毁合同、删除聊天记录或公开指控造假，可能破坏证据并引发新的名誉或违约争议。正确做法是先固定文件来源，再区分“合同是否成立”“公司是否承担表见代理责任”和“谁应承担追收责任”。

## 第一步：冻结并核验原始证据

保留纸质原件、扫描件、邮件头、聊天导出文件、签署平台日志、快递封套和文件属性。向登记机关核对印章备案、公司存续和法定代表人信息；比较同一时期真实合同的字体、编号、印文位置和签署流程。不要在原件上批注或盖骑缝章。

## 第二步：查授权与交易履行

整理授权委托书、内部审批、付款记录、发货、收货、开票及对方往来人员身份。即使印章是伪造的，实际履行、长期沉默或对方合理信赖在部分法域仍可能影响责任判断；中国法与外国法结论不能混用。

## 第三步：做专业鉴定与程序选择

根据法院、仲裁机构或当地程序要求申请司法鉴定、笔迹比对或电子数据取证。鉴定解决技术问题，不自动决定合同效力。对正在发生的付款、资产转移或追收风险，评估禁令、财产保全、确认之诉、撤销或损害赔偿请求。

## 第四步：衔接报案与民事追偿

若涉嫌诈骗、伪造印章或身份冒用，保存报案材料和受理记录，避免把刑事报案当作当然回款。同步核算已付款、货物、服务、利息和合理费用，向真正责任主体主张债权；跨境资产须按所在地程序处理。

# Forged Seals and Signatures: Contract Validity

A counterparty may present a company-sealed contract that nobody internally recognizes. Destroying it, deleting messages, or publicly alleging fraud can damage evidence and create new disputes. Separate contract formation from apparent authority and identify the person or entity responsible for recovery.

## Step 1: Preserve originals

Keep paper originals, scans, email headers, exported chats, signing-platform logs, courier packaging, and file metadata. Check company and seal records where available and compare numbering, typography, seal placement, and signing workflows with genuine contracts. Do not mark the original.

## Step 2: Review authority and performance

Collect powers of attorney, approvals, payments, shipment, receipt, invoices, and contact identities. Even a forged seal may not end the analysis: performance, silence, or reasonable reliance can affect apparent-authority issues in some jurisdictions. PRC and foreign law must be assessed separately.

## Step 3: Use the proper procedure

Seek handwriting, seal, or electronic-data examination under the court or tribunal's rules. An expert opinion addresses technical facts; it does not automatically decide validity. Consider interim relief, asset preservation, declaratory claims, avoidance, or damages where payment or dissipation is at risk.

## Step 4: Coordinate reporting and recovery

If fraud or identity misuse is suspected, preserve the report and acceptance records, but do not treat criminal reporting as a payment guarantee. Calculate payments, goods, services, interest, and reasonable costs, then pursue the responsible party. Foreign assets require local procedures.
