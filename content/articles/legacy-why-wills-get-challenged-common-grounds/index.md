# 遗嘱被质疑无效的常见原因

一份写满分配意愿的遗嘱，可能因为没有合格见证人、签署时立遗嘱人已无相应意思能力，或处分了不属于自己的财产而陷入诉讼。继承人如果先分房、转账或出售资产，事后再补证据往往困难，家庭关系和资产价值也会同步受损。

## 第一步：确认遗嘱类型与法域

核对遗嘱是在中国还是境外制作，属于自书、代书、公证、打印、录音录像或当地法认可的其他形式。中国法律与外国法律的形式要求不同；不动产通常还受所在地强制规则影响，以当地法律和专业意见为准。

## 第二步：审查签署过程

保存原件、签署视频、见证人信息、律师文件、病历和沟通记录。重点核对日期、页码、签名位置、见证人独立性以及是否存在涂改。不要只依赖继承人转述；必要时通过合法程序调取原始记录。

## 第三步：排查四类争议

一是意思能力，包括认知状态和对财产、近亲属的理解；二是欺诈、胁迫或不当影响；三是遗嘱是否处分了夫妻共同财产或他人份额；四是是否违反法定保留份、扶养义务或未成年人保护规则。跨境资产还要分别核对账户、公司股权和房产登记。

## 第四步：先保全再分配

发生争议时，暂停处分，制作资产清单，申请公证保管、遗产管理或必要的财产保全。协商应写清资产范围、税费、汇款、放弃权利和违约后果。无法和解时，再按遗产所在地与被继承人联系规则选择程序。

## 时间与费用预期（经验区间）

|事项|经验区间|费用因素|
|---|---:|---|
|遗嘱与证据初审|1–4周|文件数量与翻译|
|家事调解|1–6个月|继承人数与资产分散度|

## 风险提示

不要销毁或改动原件，不要诱导见证人统一口径，不要把“写了名字”当作当然取得全部财产。中国与外国法冲突时，应分别取得当地专业意见。

# Why Wills Get Challenged: Common Grounds

A will can express clear wishes yet fail because witnesses were unqualified, the maker lacked capacity, pressure affected the signing, or the will disposed of someone else's property. Distributing or selling assets first can make evidence harder to preserve and increase both financial and family damage.

## Step 1: Identify the form and forum

Confirm where the will was made and whether it is handwritten, witnessed, notarized, video-recorded, or another locally recognized form. Chinese and foreign requirements differ, and real estate is often subject to mandatory rules where it is located.

## Step 2: Preserve execution evidence

Keep the original, signing video, witness details, lawyer file, medical records, and communications. Check dates, page numbering, signatures, witness independence, and alterations. Do not rely only on family recollection.

## Step 3: Test four dispute areas

Review capacity; fraud, duress, or undue influence; ownership of marital or third-party property; and protected shares or support duties. For foreign assets, separately verify bank accounts, company shares, and land records.

## Step 4: Preserve before distributing

Pause transfers, prepare an asset schedule, and consider estate administration or interim protection. A settlement should cover assets, taxes, remittances, releases, and default consequences. Choose litigation or probate only after checking the governing succession and property rules.

## Time and cost expectations (experience ranges)

|Task|Typical range|Cost drivers|
|---|---:|---|
|Initial review|1–4 weeks|Documents and translation|
|Family mediation|1–6 months|Heirs and asset spread|
|Litigation or probate|6–24 months|Forum and experts|
|Foreign assets|6–30 months|Registration and compliance|

These are experience ranges. No outcome is promised.

## Risks

Do not destroy or alter originals, coach witnesses, or assume a named beneficiary receives every asset. Obtain local advice where Chinese and foreign laws differ.
