# 欠款追收的黄金时间：为什么越早行动越有利

"再等等，客户说下个月付。"——这句再等等，可能是追收失败的第一步。欠款追收有明确的黄金窗口，错过一个，难度和成本就上一个台阶。本文拆解三个关键窗口，以及每个窗口应该做的动作。

## 窗口一：逾期后30天内——黄金沟通期

刚逾期时，债务人通常还在正常经营，现金流问题刚开始显现。这个阶段：

- 双方关系还在，协商空间最大；
- 债务人未及转移资产，保全价值最高；
- 证据新鲜，沟通记录容易补全。

**行动清单**：书面确认欠款金额与原因；约定明确的付款时间表；为后续可能的法律程序留下证据。30天内解决不了的，立即升级手段。

## 窗口二：债务人经营恶化前——资产保全期

很多欠款最终收不回来，不是因为没有判决，而是因为**债务人已经没钱了**。一旦债务人出现以下信号，追收窗口正在关闭：

- 停止回复、频繁更换联系人；
- 大量裁员、停业、拖欠员工工资；
- 股东/高管变动，公司主体变更；
- 财产被其他债权人查封、被起诉记录增多。

**行动清单**：立即做资信与资产调查；考虑申请财产保全（冻结账户、查封资产）；必要时同步向其他债权人了解情况——先到先得，后到的一分钱都分不到。

## 窗口三：时效届满前——权利存续期

诉讼时效届满后，债权将丧失胜诉权（债务变为自然债务）。各法域时效不同，常见2–6年。关键点：

- 时效从"知道或应当知道权利受损"起算；
- 书面催款、债务人书面确认、部分还款都可能中断/重算时效；
- **在时效届满前启动程序或取得书面确认，是保住权利的最低成本动作**。

**行动清单**：先确认时效状态；必要时以律师函/书面确认中断时效；时效紧张时优先立案。

## 为什么越早行动越有利：数据逻辑

- 逾期6个月内采取法律行动的债权，回款概率显著高于逾期1年以上的债权；
- 财产保全在债务人资产充裕时申请，成功率远高于其资不抵债之后；
- 每一轮"再等等"，都在给对方转移资产、注销主体、拖过时效的时间。

## 给企业主的三个实操建议

1. **把"逾期天数"纳入应收账款管理**：30天、60天、90天分别对应不同动作（提醒→律师函→法律程序）；
2. **逾期即建档**：任何一笔逾期款都建立档案，含合同、凭证、沟通记录；
3. **重大欠款第一时间咨询**：金额大、涉及跨境、债务人有异常迹象的，不要自己扛。

# The Golden Window for Debt Recovery: Act Early

"Let's wait — the client says they will pay next month." That wait may be the first step toward a dead debt. Debt recovery has clear golden windows, and each missed window makes recovery harder and costlier. Here are the three windows and what to do inside each.

## Window 1: Within 30 days of default — the negotiation window

At the start of a default, the debtor is usually still operating and cash-flow problems are just emerging. At this stage:

- The relationship is intact and there is the most room to negotiate;
- Assets have not been moved — preservation value is highest;
- Evidence is fresh and communication records are easy to complete.

**Action list**: obtain a written acknowledgment of the amount and reason; agree a concrete payment schedule; preserve evidence for any later proceedings. If it is not resolved within 30 days, escalate.

## Window 2: Before the debtor's position deteriorates — the preservation window

Many debts are unrecoverable not for lack of judgments, but because **the debtor no longer has money**. Watch for these signals that the window is closing:

- No replies, changing contacts;
- Mass layoffs, closure, unpaid wages;
- Shareholder or director changes, entity restructuring;
- Property seized by other creditors, rising litigation records.

**Action list**: run credit and asset checks immediately; consider asset preservation (freezing accounts, seizing assets); talk to other creditors — first in line gets paid, latecomers get nothing.

## Window 3: Before the limitation period expires — the rights window

Once the limitation period passes, the claim loses its enforceability. Limits vary by jurisdiction, commonly 2–6 years. Key points:

- The clock runs from when the claim accrued (or when the creditor knew of it);
- Written demands, written acknowledgments, and partial payments may interrupt or restart the clock;
- **Starting proceedings or obtaining a written acknowledgment before expiry is the cheapest way to keep your rights.**

**Action list**: confirm limitation status first; interrupt the clock with a lawyer's letter or written confirmation if needed; file promptly when time is short.

## Why early action wins

- Claims pursued within 6 months of default recover at significantly higher rates than claims over a year old;
- Preservation filed while the debtor is solvent succeeds far more often than after insolvency;
- Every round of "waiting" gives the debtor time to move assets, dissolve the entity, or run out the clock.

## Three practical suggestions

1. **Build overdue days into receivables management**: 30/60/90 days trigger different actions (reminder → demand letter → proceedings);
2. **Create a file the day a payment goes overdue**: contract, vouchers, communication records;
3. **Consult early on material debts**: large, cross-border, or suspicious situations should not be handled alone.
