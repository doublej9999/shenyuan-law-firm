# 协商还款协议的法律要点（分期/担保/抵押）

客户拖欠货款数月后，突然提出“先付一小部分、以后每月再还”。如果只在聊天软件里回复同意，债权金额、利息、付款日期和违约后果都可能变成争议。等到对方再次失联，企业才发现没有可直接执行的文件，时间和诉讼成本已经增加。协商可以是务实的追收工具，但不能用含糊承诺换掉原有权利。

## 第一步：核对债权和签约主体

先把原合同、订单、发票、交付凭证、对账单、付款流水及催收记录编号整理。协议应写明本金构成、已付款项、争议金额、币种、到期日和计算方式。确认签约人有权代表公司；个人承诺与公司债务不能混写。跨境交易还要核对适用法律、语言版本、电子签名规则和签约地要求，以当地法律及专业意见为准。

## 第二步：把分期计划写成可核验的义务

列出每一期的具体金额、日期、收款账户、币种和付款手续费承担方，避免使用“资金宽裕后支付”等条件。约定付款凭证和到账认定标准，并明确部分付款先抵本金、利息还是费用。可以设置提前清偿权，但不要把经验周期写成保证。若债务人要求减免，建议将减免写成履约后的附条件优惠：未按期付款时，优惠是否失效必须清楚。

## 第三步：安排担保、抵押与保证责任

保证条款应写明保证人、保证范围、保证期间、责任类型及通知方式；不同法域对保证形式和期间要求不同。抵押或质押不能只写“以资产担保”，应核实资产权属、优先权、登记机关、评估价值和设立手续。海外资产需要当地律师确认登记与执行可行性。担保价值会变化，不能把名义估值当作足额保障，也不要接受来源不明的资产资料。

## 第四步：连接违约、加速与程序救济

明确一期逾期多久构成违约、是否触发全部余额到期、违约金或逾期利息如何计算，以及催告和补救期限。约定争议解决条款时，检查其与原合同是否冲突，避免误写成放弃仲裁、诉讼或保全权利。协议最好确认原债权不因谈判被默示免除；债务确认也不能当然中断或延长所有法域的时效，需单独核对。

## 风险提示

- 不要用收款截图代替到账核验，也不要让无授权人员签署债务确认。
- 不要未经审查就接受第三方代偿、空泛保证或无法登记的抵押。
- 不要因签分期协议而自动放弃保全、仲裁、诉讼或执行权利。
- 中国法律与外国法规则可能不同，时效和担保效力以当地专业意见为准。

# Drafting Settlement Agreements: Installments and Guarantees

A debtor may offer a small payment today and promise monthly installments later. If the arrangement remains in chat messages, the balance, due dates, interest, and consequences of default may all become disputed. By the time the debtor disappears again, the business may lack a clear document for relief and face higher costs. A settlement can be useful, but it should not trade away existing rights for vague promises.

## Step 1: Verify the debt and signing authority

Organize the contract, orders, invoices, delivery records, statements, payment records, and collection correspondence. State the principal, payments already made, disputed amount, currency, maturity date, and calculation method. Confirm that each signatory can bind the company; do not confuse a director's personal promise with corporate liability. Check governing law, language versions, e-signature requirements, and execution formalities in each relevant jurisdiction.

## Step 2: Make every installment measurable

List the exact amount, date, currency, receiving account, and fee allocation for each installment. Avoid conditions such as “when cash flow improves.” Define proof of payment and when payment is treated as received. State whether a partial payment reduces principal, interest, or costs. Any discount should preferably be conditional on full and timely performance, with clear consequences if an installment is missed.

## Step 3: Document guarantees and security

Identify the guarantor, secured obligations, duration, notice method, and type of liability. Guarantee formalities and limitation periods vary by jurisdiction. For a mortgage or pledge, verify title, priorities, registration, valuation, and perfection steps rather than merely writing “assets are security.” Local counsel should confirm whether overseas security can be registered and enforced. Nominal value is not the same as recoverable value.

## Step 4: Link default to enforcement

Define when a missed installment becomes default, whether it accelerates the balance, and how interest, charges, notice, and cure periods operate. Check that the dispute clause is consistent with the original contract and does not unintentionally waive arbitration, litigation, or preservation remedies. A debt acknowledgment does not automatically extend or interrupt limitation periods in every jurisdiction; obtain specific advice.
