# 股权代持与继承纠纷

陈某当年出全资入股，却出于身份或个人原因，把股份登记在了好友名下。如今陈某去世，好友却称"这股权本来就是我名下的"，甚至想把股份转卖。继承人手上只有一份代持协议，公司登记名册里根本没有逝者的名字。如果不及时确权，代持人可能转移股权、注销公司，继承资格和证据都会一点点作废。股权代持与继承怎么处理，请你往下看。

## 第一步：先判断代持协议是否有效

- 股权代持（委托持股）协议原则上有效，但涉及特殊行业（如金融、证券等需资质准入的领域）可能被认定无效或受限；
- 是否有书面代持协议、实际出资凭证（转账记录、流水、收据）是关键证据；仅有口头约定则举证更难；
- 需注意代持协议与中国公司法、公司章程的衔接，境外持股还涉及当地法律，拿不准的以当地法律/专业意见为准。

## 第二步：确认"实际权利人"身份

- 继承纠纷的前提是"先确权、后继承"：继承人通常要先证明逝者才是股权的**实际出资人/受益人**，再谈继承份额；
- 若代持人否认，可通过**股东资格确认之诉**（隐名股东确权）要求确认实际权利人及其股权比例；
- 固定证据链：代持协议、出资凭证、分红记录、历次股东会/决策中的实际参与情况，形成"谁出资、谁收益、谁管理"的完整闭环。

## 第三步：合法权益如何由继承人接续

- 确权成功后，继承人在合法继承范围内取得相应股权权利，可要求公司配合办理变更登记与**过户**；
- 若客观条件使直接过户不便，可先通过遗嘱或继承协议约定权益分配，再逐步推进工商/公司登记变更；
- 同股权的其他继承人、公司其他股东若提出异议（如优先购买权、章程限制），需要一并协商或通过法定程序处理。

## 第四步：同步处理税费与登记

- 股权继承通常涉及所得税/继承税或相关登记费用，具体税负因"继承人身份"与"遗产所在地法"而异，应提前测算；
- 跨境情形（逝者在境外、公司在境内或反之）还要处理公证、认证与外汇合规，程序更复杂；
- 建议在确权与过户前先咨询当地税务与登记实务，避免因文件缺失被反复退回。

## 时间与费用预期

| 路径 | 经验周期 | 主要成本 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 确认出资与代持协议 | 数天至1个月 | 低（证据梳理） |
| 股东资格确认之诉 | 4–12个月不等 | 律师费+诉讼费 |
| 继承确权与调解 | 2–8个月不等 | 调解/律师费 |
| 变更登记与过户 | 数周至数月 | 登记费+税费 |

以上均为经验区间，实际周期与成本取决于代持关系是否清晰、代持人态度与所在法域。我们不承诺结果，但"先确权再继承、越早固定证据"通常能显著降低风险。

## 风险提示

- 不要认为"合同签了就万无一失"，代持关系若被认定无效或代持人恶意转移，风险很高；
- 不要拖着不确权，代持人一旦转让或处置股权，追回难度会急剧上升；
- 不要忽略中国法律规定与实际履行地的差异，股权继承常同时牵涉公司法和继承法；
- 缴税、认证、外汇等环节复杂，务必以当地法律/专业意见为准，切勿自行简化。

# Inheriting Beneficially-Owned (Nominee) Shares

Chen funded the entire equity purchase but, for identity or personal reasons, registered the shares in a friend's name. Chen has since died, and the friend now claims, "These shares were always in my name," and may even try to sell them. The heirs hold only the nominee agreement; the company register bears no trace of Chen. Unless rights are confirmed promptly, the nominee may transfer the equity or wind up the company, and the inheritance claim and the evidence erode together. Here is how to handle nominee shareholding when the owner dies.

## Step 1: assess whether the nominee agreement is valid

- A nominee/entrusted-shareholding arrangement is generally valid, but in industries with licensing thresholds (e.g., finance, securities) it may be void or restricted;
- A written agreement plus actual funding records (bank transfers, flows, receipts) are the decisive evidence; an oral arrangement is far harder to prove;
- Note the interaction with China's Company Law and the company's articles; overseas holdings also raise local-law issues, so follow local law/professional advice where unclear.

## Step 2: establish who the actual beneficial owner is

- In inheritance disputes the sequence is "confirm rights first, then inherit": heirs usually must prove the deceased was the **true contributor/beneficial owner** of the equity before claiming a share;
- If the nominee denies it, bring an **equity-confirmation action** (hidden shareholder rights) to establish the beneficial owner and their share percentage;
- Build the evidence chain: nominee agreement, funding vouchers, dividend records, and evidence of actual involvement in board/shareholder decisions—forming a complete "who funded, who benefited, who managed" picture.

## Step 3: how the heirs take over the rights

- Once confirmed, heirs acquire the corresponding equity rights within the lawful inheritance scope and may require the company to cooperate in registration change and **transfer of title**;
- If a direct transfer is inconvenient, first fix the interest distribution by will or inheritance agreement, then progress the company/commercial registration in stages;
- If other heirs or shareholders object (e.g., rights of first refusal, articles' restrictions), address these together or through the statutory process.

## Step 4: handle taxes and registration at the same time

- Inheriting shares generally triggers income/inheritance tax or registration fees, which vary by the heirs' status and the situs of assets—assess these early;
- Cross-border cases (owner overseas, company in China, or vice versa) add notarization, apostille, and foreign-exchange compliance, making the process more complex;
- Check local tax and registration practice before confirming rights and transferring title, to avoid repeated rejections over missing documents.

## Time and cost expectations

| Route | Typical timeline | Main costs |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| Confirm funding and nominee agreement | Days to 1 month | Low (evidence) |
| Equity-confirmation action | 4–12 months, varies | Counsel + court fees |
| Inheritance confirmation / mediation | 2–8 months, varies | Mediation/counsel |
| Registration change and transfer | Weeks to months | Registration fees + taxes |

These are experience ranges; actual timelines and costs depend on how clear the nominee relationship is, the nominee's attitude, and the jurisdiction. We do not promise results, but "confirm rights before inheriting and fix evidence early" generally lowers risk substantially.

## Key risks

- Do not assume a signed contract is foolproof; if the nominee relationship is void or the nominee acts in bad faith, the risk is high;
- Do not delay confirmation—once the nominee transfers or disposes of the equity, recovery becomes much harder;
- Do not overlook the gap between China law and the place of performance; share inheritance implicates both company law and inheritance law;
- Tax, certification, and foreign-exchange steps are intricate—follow local law/professional advice rather than shortcutting.
