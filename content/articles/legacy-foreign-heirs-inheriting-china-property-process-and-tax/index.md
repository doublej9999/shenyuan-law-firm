# 中国房产由海外继承人继承：流程与税费

父母在国内的房改房或商品房，登记在已故父母名下，而你在海外长期定居。想办继承过户，却不知道从哪一步开始：是先公证继承还是先去不动产登记中心？海外身份的材料怎么认证？卖了房子钱怎么合法汇出？很多人耗了大半年，材料来回被退回，最后才发现顺序走反了、遗漏了关键文件。房产继承里，程序顺序错了，后续全部卡壳。

## 第1步：确认继承方式与继承人范围

- 有遗嘱的，先确认遗嘱效力并办理遗嘱继承；无遗嘱的，按法定继承顺序确定全部继承人；
- 核实继承人原国籍/居留地、婚姻与子女情况，明确哪些继承人需要到场或可委托办理；
- 继承人之间就房产归属达成一致的，可先行协商分割再办理。

## 第2步：办理国外文件的公证认证

- 海外出具的身份证件、死亡证明、亲属关系证明等，通常需办理公证及领事认证或海牙认证（Apostille）；
- 认证机构与份数以不动产所在地登记部门要求为准，务必提前问清清单；
- 提交中文翻译件，翻译件通常亦需由有资质机构出具。

## 第3步：办理继承权公证（视当地要求）

- 多数地区不动产继承需先取得继承权公证书作为登记依据；
- 全部继承人在公证机构到场或通过合规方式确认放弃或接受继承；
- 若继承人无法到场，可办理授权委托并公证认证。

## 第4步：办理不动产变更登记

- 携带继承权公证书、认证文件、原产权证、身份证明等到房产所在地不动产登记中心申请继承转移登记；
- 登记审核无误后将权利人变更为继承人，领取新的不动产权证书；
- 各地审核口径有差异，可提前就材料清单做预沟通。

## 第5步：涉及出售或汇出时的税务与外汇

- 继承环节本身通常不单独征收"继承税"，主要涉及契税、登记费等，具体以当地规定为准；
- 若出售继承房产，需关注个人所得税等税负，转让价格与计税依据按政策核定；
- 售房所得如需汇往境外，按规定办理购付汇并向银行提供继承、完税等证明材料。

## 时间与费用预期

| 环节 | 经验周期 | 主要成本 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 材料认证与翻译 | 1–3 个月 | 公证/认证/翻译费 |
| 继承权公证 | 1–2 个月 | 公证费（按标的） |
| 不动产变更登记 | 2 周–2 个月 | 登记费+契税（如适用） |
| 出售/汇出安排 | 1–6 个月 | 税费+银行手续费 |

以上均为经验区间，各地材料与时限可能有差异。我们不承诺结果，但按正确顺序、提早备齐材料，能显著减少来回折腾。

## 风险提示

- 不要忽略任一继承人的意思表示，遗漏会导致登记被拒或引发纠纷；
- 不要提供不实的亲属/继承关系证明，可能被认定为无效并承担法律责任；
- 出售与汇出环节的税务、外汇政策会调整，务必以办理时点的现行规定为准；
- 夫妻共同财产、遗嘱真实性、外国法认证等问题复杂时，请以当地法律及专业意见为准。

# Foreign Heirs Inheriting China Property: Process and Tax

Your parents' apartment in China is registered in the deceased parents' names, while you live overseas long-term. You want to complete the inheritance transfer but do not know where to begin: notarize the succession first, or go to the land registry? How should your foreign-issued documents be authenticated? And if you sell, how do you legally remit the proceeds? Many people spend the better part of a year, only to have documents rejected repeatedly because steps were done out of order or key papers were missing. In property inheritance, getting the sequence wrong stalls everything downstream.

## Step 1: Confirm the succession route and the heirs

- If there is a will, confirm its validity and proceed by testate succession; otherwise follow the statutory order and identify all heirs.
- Verify each heir's nationality or residence, marriage, and children, and decide who must appear in person or may be represented.
- If heirs agree on who takes the property, settle the split before proceeding.

## Step 2: Notarize and authenticate foreign documents

- Identity documents, death certificates, and kinship certificates issued abroad usually need notarization plus consular legalization or an apostille.
- Confirm the required number of sets with the registry in the property's location beforehand.
- Provide Chinese translations; translations are usually also required from qualified providers.

## Step 3: Obtain a succession certificate where required

- In most regions, transferring real estate requires a succession (inheritance) certificate as the registration basis.
- All heirs confirm acceptance or waiver at the notary, in person or through a compliant process.
- If an heir cannot appear, complete a power of attorney and authenticate it.

## Step 4: Apply for the ownership transfer

- Take the succession certificate, authenticated documents, the original title deed, and identity papers to the local real estate registry to apply for the transfer.
- Once verified, title is changed to the heirs and a new certificate issues.
- Practice varies by locality; pre-confirming the document list helps avoid rejections.

## Step 5: Tax and FX matters if selling

- The inheritance step itself normally does not attract a separate "inheritance tax" in China; the main items are deed tax and registration fees, subject to local rules.
- If you sell the inherited property, monitor income tax and related liabilities; the taxable basis follows current policy.
- To remit sale proceeds abroad, follow the purchase-and-remittance procedures and provide the bank with inheritance and tax-payment documents.

## Time and cost expectations

| Stage | Typical range | Main costs |
|-------|---------------|------------|
| Authentication and translation | 1–3 months | Notary, legalization, translation |
| Succession certificate | 1–2 months | Notary fee (ad valorem) |
| Ownership transfer | 2 weeks–2 months | Registration and deed tax (if any) |
| Sale / remittance | 1–6 months | Taxes and bank fees |

These are experience ranges; local requirements vary. We do not promise outcomes, but following the correct order and preparing documents early significantly reduces back-and-forth.

## Key risks

- Do not overlook any heir's consent — an omission can void the registration or spark disputes.
- Do not submit false kinship or inheritance evidence; it may be found invalid and carry liability.
- Tax and FX rules change — rely on the rules in force at the time of the transaction.
- Where community property, will validity, or foreign-law authentication is complex, follow local law and professional advice.
