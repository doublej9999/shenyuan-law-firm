# 海外客户破产了，货款还能要回来吗？

货柜已经进港，尾款还差四成没到账，突然收到客户所在地法院寄来的破产通知：对方资不抵债，进入破产清算。你第一反应是"完了，钱打水漂了"。但先别急着躺平——破产不等于一分钱都要不回来，关键在于你进场够不够快、动作够不够对。拖到债权申报截止日之后才动手，可能连"债权人"的身份都保不住。

## 先分清你的处境

- 已发货、货款未付：你属于破产企业的债权人，需申报债权；
- 货还在途中、客户破产：可考虑行使停运权或中途货物留置权（视适用法是否支持而定）；
- 有物权保留条款且依约保留所有权：货物可能上不属于破产财产，主张路径完全不同；
- 有担保、抵押或母公司连带担保：可优先受偿，务必单独申报并注意时效。

## 第1步：第一时间止损与核实

- 立即暂停向该客户继续发货，评估在途货物能否保全；
- 收到破产通知后务必确认破产程序类型（重整还是清算）与申报截止日期，错过即丧失受偿资格；
- 保留全部合同、订单、提单、发票与往来记录，固定债权金额与证据。

## 第2步：及时申报债权

- 在截止日前向破产管理人正式申报债权，附合同与单据；
- 若属优先债权（担保债权、破产管理人未清偿的特定费用等），单独主张优先顺位；
- 金额务必准确，虚高申报可能被核减并损害可信度。

## 第3步：评估担保、保证与追索路径

- 是否存在母公司/个人担保、出口信用保险或保理安排，第一时间向相关方主张；
- 若已投保出口信用保险，尽快按保单要求报案并提供损失单据，注意保单约定的申报与索赔期限；
- 对与中国有司法协助或商业往来的债务人/保证人，评估在境内另行追收的可能。

## 第4步：参与破产分配与后续执行

- 关注管理人报告与债权人会议，必要时通过当地律师质询资产去向，防止被低价处置；
- 若破产程序被滥用或存在欺诈性转移，可通过当地法律途径主张撤销。

## 时间与费用预期

| 环节 | 经验周期 | 主要成本 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 债权申报与核验 | 1–3 个月 | 律师费+管理人申报费 |
| 破产分配/重整 | 6 个月–3 年 | 律师费+管理人费用分担 |
| 担保/保险追偿 | 3–12 个月 | 按保单或合同约定 |
| 境内另行追索 | 3–18 个月 | 诉讼/仲裁与执行费用 |

以上均为经验区间，结果视破产财产规模与顺位而定。我们不承诺结果，但申报越及时、权利主张越完整，回收概率与比例越高。

## 风险提示

- 不要因为恐慌把未到期的应收账款低价转让给不明资方，谨防"债务收购"骗局；
- 不要错过申报截止日——这是最常见的致命错误；
- 不要擅自扣留与本案无关的客户其他货物，可能构成违约并招致反索赔；
- 各国破产法差异很大，是否承认担保、停运权与优先顺位，一律以当地法律及专业意见为准。

# Buyer Bankruptcy: Can You Still Recover Your Export Debt?

Your container has arrived at port, four-tenths of the balance is still unpaid, and suddenly a bankruptcy notice arrives from the buyer's local court: the customer is insolvent and entering liquidation. Your first reaction may be "the money is gone." But before giving up, remember that bankruptcy does not always mean a total loss — it depends on how fast you act and whether you assert your rights correctly. Miss the deadline for filing a claim, and you may lose even your status as a creditor.

## First, identify your position

- Goods delivered and unpaid: you are a creditor of the insolvent company and must file a claim.
- Goods still in transit: consider stoppage in transit or an unpaid seller's lien, where supported by the applicable law.
- A retention-of-title clause was agreed and title is retained: the goods may fall outside the bankruptcy estate entirely.
- Security, collateral, or a parent-level guarantee exists: you may have priority, but you still must file in time.

## Step 1: Stop losses and verify immediately

- Halt further deliveries to the buyer and assess whether in-transit goods can be preserved.
- Confirm the type of proceeding (reorganization or liquidation) and the claim-filing deadline; missing it forfeits entitlement.
- Preserve all contracts, orders, bills of lading, invoices, and correspondence to fix the amount and the evidence base.

## Step 2: File your claim on time

- File a formal claim with the insolvency administrator before the deadline, attaching contracts and documents.
- If you hold priority rights (e.g. secured claims), claim that priority separately.
- State the amount accurately; inflation can be challenged and damages your credibility.

## Step 3: Assess guarantees, insurance, and other routes

- Identify any parent or personal guarantees, export credit insurance, or factoring arrangements, and pursue them promptly.
- If insured, notify and file under the policy quickly, observing the required documentation and claim deadlines.
- Where the debtor or guarantor has assets or ties in China, evaluate parallel recovery there.

## Step 4: Participate and follow up

- Follow the administrator's reports and creditor meetings; through local counsel, scrutinize asset disposition to prevent undervaluation.
- If the bankruptcy is abused or assets are fraudulently transferred, pursue set-aside under local law.

## Time and cost expectations

| Stage | Typical range | Main costs |
|-------|---------------|------------|
| Claim filing and verification | 1–3 months | Counsel and administrator fees |
| Distribution / reorganization | 6 months–3 years | Counsel fees and fee sharing |
| Guarantee / insurance recovery | 3–12 months | As per policy or contract |
| Parallel recovery in China | 3–18 months | Litigation, arbitration and enforcement costs |

These are experience ranges and depend on the size of the estate and your priority. We do not promise outcomes, but the timelier and more complete your claims, the better the recovery odds and ratio.

## Key risks

- Do not panic-sell receivables to an unverified "debt buyer" — acquisition scams are common.
- Do not miss the filing deadline — it is the most frequent fatal error.
- Do not seize unrelated goods of the customer; it may breach your contract and invite a cross-claim.
- Bankruptcy laws differ sharply by country — recognition of guarantees, stoppage rights, and priority always follows local law and professional advice.
